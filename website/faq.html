Questions installers ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, email hello@velarqo.com.

Where did you get my email address?

From public sources: Companies House, your own website and map listings. We only email limited companies at a business address, we say who we are in every email, and if you reply "no" we never email you again. The details are in our privacy policy.

What do you send my customers?

Up to 3 short texts and 1 email per homeowner over about 2 weeks, in your firm's name, from a UK number, Monday to Saturday between 9am and 7pm. Each one is a plain check-in about the quote they asked you for. You approve every word before anything goes out. Anyone who replies STOP is never contacted again. See the messages.

Is this GDPR-OK?

You stay the data controller. Velarqo works as your processor under a written data processing agreement signed before we start. Your customers' data is used only for your campaign, never sold or shared with anyone else, and deleted 30 days after the campaign ends unless you continue. Opt-outs are honoured permanently, and we send you anyone who asks to see or delete their data within 2 working days.

What counts as a booked survey?

A homeowner from one of your old quotes who, in reply to our messages, confirms a date and time for a survey in your calendar, for something you do, at an address in your area. "Send me some info" or "maybe" does not count. A booking that is moved counts once. One charge per person, even if they had two quotes. If you can show you were already in touch with them about this job in the 90 days before we messaged them, it is yours, not ours.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. You pay a fee for each survey that gets booked, agreed on the phone before anything starts and written into the pilot terms. If the homeowner is not in when your surveyor arrives, that one is credited on the next invoice. Invoices are weekly, and you set a cap on how many surveys you want.

What if we are already booked up?

Then this is probably not for you right now, and I will say so. It only makes sense if you want more surveys in the diary. If you have a quiet month coming, you choose the survey slots and the batch size, and we can start small or pause at any time.

What data do you need from me?

An export of quotes from the last 3 months to 2 years that did not become a job, and your do-not-contact list. Useful columns are the quote date, what it was for, rough value, status and where the enquiry came from. For the free audit you can blank out names and numbers. Whatever your spreadsheet, CRM or quoting software gives you is fine.

How long does it take?

Within 2 working days of your export I tell you how many quotes look worth chasing. Once you have approved the messages, the first batch of 50 to 100 homeowners goes out, each getting the 2-week sequence. A pilot runs about 6 weeks from the first message. Replies are answered within 1 working hour.
