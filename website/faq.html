Where did you get my email address?

From public sources: Companies House, your own website and map listings. I only email limited companies at a business address, I say who I am in every email, and if you reply "no" I never email you again. The details are in the privacy policy.

What do you send my customers?

Up to 3 short texts and 1 email per homeowner over about 2 weeks, in your firm's name, from a UK number, Monday to Saturday between 9am and 7pm. Each one is a plain check-in about the quote they asked you for. You approve every word before anything goes out. Anyone who replies STOP is never contacted again. See the messages.

They're my leads. Why would I pay you for them?

You'd be paying for booked surveys from quotes nobody is chasing, not for leads. I only touch quotes that are 3 months to 2 years old and weren't won, and anyone who has opted out is left alone. If your team already rings every old quote back, you don't need me. I also keep a slice of your list back, uncontacted, so you can see what the follow-up added compared with doing nothing.

Won't it annoy my customers?

They asked you for a quote, and every message is a short check-in about that quote, from your firm. One STOP, or any reply asking to be left alone, and they never hear from you again. Anything unusual, a complaint or a sensitive reply, comes straight to you the same working day, not to a template.

Is this GDPR-OK?

You stay the data controller. Velarqo works as your processor under a written data processing agreement signed before we start. Your customers' data is used only for your follow-up, never sold or shared with anyone else, and deleted within 30 days of the campaign ending unless you continue, apart from the do-not-contact list, which is kept so nobody who said no hears from you again. Opt-outs are honoured permanently, and anyone who asks to see or delete their data is passed to you within 2 working days.

What counts as a booked survey?

A homeowner from one of your old quotes who, in reply to my messages, confirms a date and time for a survey in your calendar, for something you do, at an address in your area. "Send me some info" or "maybe" does not count. A booking that is moved counts once. One charge per person, even if they had two quotes. If you can show you were already in touch with them about this job in the 90 days before I messaged them, it is yours, not mine.

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. You pay a fee for each survey that gets booked, agreed on a call before anything starts and written into the terms. If the homeowner is not in when your surveyor arrives, that one is credited on the next invoice. Invoices are weekly, and you set a cap on how many surveys you want.

How do I know it works?

You don't yet, and neither do I on your quotes. Velarqo is new and has no results to show you. That's why it starts with a small batch, you approve every word, and you only pay for surveys that get booked. For the first few firms, the first 50 old quotes are chased free.

Can you guarantee sales?

No. I can't control your survey or your price, so I don't charge for them. I only charge for what I can deliver: the booked survey.

What if we are already booked up?

Then this is probably not for you right now, and I will say so. It only makes sense if you want more surveys in the diary: next season, or the gaps when a job falls through. You choose the survey slots and the batch size, and we can start small or pause at any time.

What data do you need from me?

An export of quotes from the last 3 months to 2 years that did not become a job, and your do-not-contact list. Useful columns are the quote date, what it was for, rough value, status and where the enquiry came from. For the free look you can blank out names and numbers. Whatever your spreadsheet, CRM or quoting software gives you is fine, and if it's a mess, that's normal: send a small sample first.

How long does it take?

Within 2 working days of your export I tell you how many quotes look worth chasing. Once you have approved the messages, the first batch of 50 to 100 homeowners goes out, each getting the 2-week sequence. A pilot runs about 6 weeks from the first message. Replies are answered within 1 working hour.

Who is behind Velarqo?

Me, Pablo Garcia. Velarqo is a one-person service, based in Spain and working UK hours. I set up every follow-up and answer every reply myself. There is no account manager and no call centre: hello@velarqo.com comes straight to me.
