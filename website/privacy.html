Privacy & legal

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who runs Velarqo

Velarqo is run by Pablo, an individual based in Spain. Contact: hello@velarqo.com. Pablo is the data controller for the personal data described here. Full legal identification details will be published on this page once the business is registered.

Business contacts we reach out to

We contact UK window and door companies that may benefit from our service. We collect business contact details from public sources, including Companies House, OpenStreetMap and the company's own website. This can include the company name, business address, business email address, phone number and the name of a director.

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: introducing a relevant service to businesses. Every message identifies us and explains how to opt out. If you ask us to stop, we stop, and keep only the minimum record needed to make sure we don't contact you again.

People who contact us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and whatever you send to reply and, if you become a client, to deliver our service. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests or taking steps to enter into a contract.

Our clients' customer data

When a client asks us to follow up their old quotes, we process their customers' data only on the client's instructions, as a data processor under a written data processing agreement. The client remains the controller. If you've received a message from us on behalf of a company that quoted you, reply to opt out or contact that company directly.

Who we share data with

We don't sell personal data. We use service providers to run the business, such as email, hosting and database providers. Where they store data outside the UK or EU, appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses are in place.

How long we keep it

Prospecting data is kept for up to 24 months from collection, unless you become a client or ask us to stop sooner. Client records are kept as long as the contract requires plus any period the law requires for tax and accounting.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected or erased
  • object to or restrict how we use it, including objecting to direct marketing at any time
  • data portability, where it applies

To use any of these rights, email hello@velarqo.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
