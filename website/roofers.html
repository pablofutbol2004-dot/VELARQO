Notes

A roof that needed doing last year still needs doing.

Re-roofs, flat roofs, fascias and guttering: homeowners put them off until the next leak. Your quote sits in a drawer while they wait for a dry summer or a bit more money.

When they finally decide, they call whoever is in front of them. A short, polite check-in from your firm puts you back in front of them, without you spending evenings on the phone.
