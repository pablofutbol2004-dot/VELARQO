Notes

A quote that went quiet is a lead you've already paid for.

You took the enquiry and priced the job. The homeowner got busy, the budget moved, another quote landed first. Nobody said no.

Months later, some of them may still need the work. Following up hundreds of old quotes by hand rarely makes it to the top of the list.

Terms

  1. Your customers are treated properly.

    Every message is a short, relevant check-in about a quote they asked for. Anyone who isn't interested is removed and never contacted again.

  2. Your data stays yours.

    It's used only to run your campaign, kept secure, and never sold or used for anything else. The services we use to run it are listed in the privacy policy. We sign a data processing agreement before we start. See the privacy policy.

  3. Older quotes too.

    We look at quotes from about 3 months to 2 years old. Newer ones you're probably still chasing yourself, so we leave them alone.

  4. You deal with one person.

    Velarqo is new and deliberately small. You talk to the person doing the work, not an account manager.
