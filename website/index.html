What happens, start to finish.

  1. You send me your old quotes.

    A spreadsheet or an export from your quoting software. Quotes from 3 months to 2 years old that didn't go ahead. Messy is fine. Within 2 working days I tell you honestly whether they're worth chasing.

  2. You approve every word.

    You see the exact texts and the one email your customers will get, with your firm's name on them. Nothing goes out until you say yes.

  3. Surveys go in your diary.

    I send the messages over about two weeks, read every reply and offer the people still interested a time from the slots you gave me. You go out and quote as normal.

What your customers receive.

Short, plain, and from your firm. The words in brackets are filled in from your quote records. You can change any of it.

  • Three texts and one email at most, over about two weeks.
  • Sent 9am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday. Never on a Sunday.
  • It stops the moment someone replies, books or says STOP. Anyone who says no is never contacted again.
  • No discounts or made-up deadlines, unless you have a real offer you want to run.

The questions installers ask first.

"They're my leads. Why would I pay you for them?"

You wouldn't be paying for leads. I only touch quotes that are 3 months to 2 years old and weren't won: the ones nobody is chasing. If your team already rings every old quote, you don't need me. I also keep a slice of your list back, uncontacted, so you can see what the follow-up added compared with doing nothing.

"Won't it annoy my customers?"

They asked you for a quote, and the messages are short and about that quote. One STOP and they never hear from you again. You read and approve every word before a single one goes out.

"Is this GDPR-OK?"

You stay in charge of your customers' data. I work as your processor under a written data processing agreement, signed before we start. Your data is used only for your follow-up, never sold, and deleted within 30 days of the campaign ending, apart from the do-not-contact list, which is kept so nobody who said no hears from you again.

"Where did you get my email?"

From Companies House and your own website. I only email limited companies at their business address. Reply "no" to any email and I won't email you again. Details are in the privacy policy.

"We're booked up for months."

Then this may not be for you right now, and I'll say so. It's for filling next season and the gaps when a job falls through, from people who already know your prices. You decide how many surveys a week, and you can pause any time.

"How do I know it works?"

You don't yet, and neither do I on your quotes. Velarqo is new. That's why it starts small, you approve everything, and you only pay for surveys that get booked. Booked, not sold: I can't control your price or your survey, so I don't charge for them.

"What does it cost?"

A fee for each booked survey, agreed on a call before anything starts and written into the terms. Nothing upfront. If the homeowner isn't in when you turn up, that one comes off the next invoice.
