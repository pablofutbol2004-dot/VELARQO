## Scope

velarqo.com landing site (home, three trade pages, FAQ, privacy, 404). Mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job

UK window/door installer, roofer or kitchen fitter owner, 40-60, on a phone between jobs, arrived by googling Velarqo after a cold email with no links. Job: decide in 30 seconds whether a real person with a credible, low-risk offer is behind this, then reply to the email or email Pablo. Proof available: none (pre-revenue). Proof by showing: the exact homeowner texts, the exact steps, the founder's name and face (photo pending).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the installer's own desk diary: quotes that went quiet become survey bookings written into next week. It refuses the agency hero (gradient, stock handshake, stats band) and the old dark quotation-form costume.

OWN-WORLD: White page, near-black ink (#16181b), hairline diary rules in cool grey, brick red (#b3261e, logo-derived) used only for day headings, the booked-survey entry and the one button. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next throughout, 19px body, 800-weight headings. Components: week-to-view grid, day-heading rule, SMS bubbles in plain grey, a red handwritten-feel booking block (set type, not script).

STORY: They understand it works their own old quotes, see the exact text their customer gets, see they pay only per booked survey and approve every word, meet the one person doing it, and email or reply.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: logo + email link bar; headline "Quotes that went quiet, back in your diary as surveys." at ~38px; one sentence; red button "Email Pablo"; below the fold edge, the diary card begins. FORM: Desk diary (week-to-view), position 7 of the grounded list, seed key c32319a6. Signature interaction: the example booking writes itself into Thursday once on load (clip-path reveal), static under reduced motion.
