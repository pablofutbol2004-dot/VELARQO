# Red team for new Velarqo ideas

Written 11 Oct 2026, after dropping installers. Every new idea goes through
this before we pick it. Paste section 4 into ChatGPT so its ideas arrive
pre-filtered.

Deadline: pick by **Fri 16 Oct**. First send ~1 Nov.

## 1. Hard gates (one "no" kills the idea)

| # | Gate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | We sell to **UK limited companies or LLPs** (not sole traders or partnerships) | PECR lets us cold email "corporate subscribers"; sole traders count as individuals |
| L2 | Delivering the service never means emailing, texting or calling **consumers** without their consent | The installer lesson: the soft opt-in almost never exists |
| L3 | Not a regulated activity: no financial, debt, insurance, legal, medical or immigration advice; no claims management | Needs FCA/SRA authorisation or carries liability we can't carry |
| L4 | No buying, selling or renting personal data | Commodity anyway; PECR/UK GDPR risk |
| D1 | Pablo can deliver it from Spain, by email/async, in **10-20 h a week**, no phone calls | Founder hours are the ceiling; exams exist |
| D2 | No big software build and no paid tools before the first sale | Free-tier rule; ship fast |
| D3 | Pablo (plus AI) can do it well enough to get a result in a 30-day pilot | Otherwise no case study |
| M1 | The buyer **loses money from this problem every month** and knows it | Vitamins don't get replies |
| M2 | Can be **monthly, ≥£300/month** | One-offs don't build income |
| M3 | Someone already pays for it today (competitor with public prices, job ads, Upwork/agency offers) | Proves budget exists; no competitor usually means no market |
| R1 | We can build **≥3,000 firms** from free sources (Companies House SIC codes, Google Places) | The engine needs volume to test |
| R2 | The decision-maker reads email (owner-led firm, 2-50 staff) | Our only channel |

## 2. Red team (only for ideas that pass every gate)

1. **Pre-mortem:** it's Oct 2027 and it died. Top 5 causes, each with the
   earliest warning sign.
2. **Check every claim** the idea leans on. Label each: VERIFIED (primary
   source opened), VENDOR (someone selling into the market), ANECDOTAL,
   GUESS.
3. **Competitors:** who sells it, at what price, how many clients. Why would
   a firm pick a student in Spain over them?
4. **Per-client maths:** fee, hours to deliver, value to the client. Is it
   an easy yes on the numbers?
5. **Funnel:** how many sales steps from cold email to money? Fewer is better.
6. **Legal pass:** walk through delivery step by step and name who gets
   contacted, how, and on what legal basis.

## 3. Scoring (top 2 only)

Score 1-5 each: pain (money lost), willingness to pay (evidence), ease of
delivery, legal cleanliness, list size, speed to first result, fit with the
engine. Pick the highest total; a tie goes to the one with fewer sales steps.

## 4. Prompt to paste into ChatGPT

> I run a cold email engine (8 warmed Google Workspace inboxes, UK company
> lists from Companies House and Google Places). I'm a student in Spain with
> 10-20 hours a week, no phone sales, no budget before the first sale. Suggest
> B2B services I can sell to UK limited companies (2-50 staff) where: the
> buyer loses money from the problem every month; I can deliver it by email
> and with AI, remotely; it can be £300+/month; someone already sells it
> today; delivering it never requires contacting consumers without consent;
> it's not regulated (no financial, legal, medical, debt, insurance advice).
> For each idea give: the buyer, the problem, what I'd deliver, who already
> sells it and at what price, and the biggest reason it could fail.
