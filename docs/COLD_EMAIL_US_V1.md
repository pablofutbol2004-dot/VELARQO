# Cold email: US estimate recovery, v1 draft (12 Oct 2026)

Rules (from `PLAN_US_2026-10.md` and the reviews):
- Third-grade reading level, under 80 words.
- One question, one small ask.
- No claims we can't prove, and no fake "Re:".
- The engine adds the footer automatically: name, business, postal address, opt-out.
- Two emails per firm. Follow-up 2 goes out 3 business days later in the same thread.

Placeholders: `{first_name}` (fallback "there"), `{company}`, `{city}`, `{sender_first}`.

## Remodelers (kitchen & bath)

**Email 1**
Subject: `{company}'s old estimates`

> Hi {first_name},
>
> How many kitchen or bath estimates did {company} send this year that never got a yes or a no?
>
> Some of those homeowners are still planning. They just went quiet.
>
> I can write the 3 follow-up emails I'd send them, with {company}'s name on them. They're free, and you don't need to get on a call. Want them?
>
> {sender_first}

**Follow-up 2**

> Hi {first_name}, should I write those 3 emails for {company}?
>
> If you'd rather not do it yourself, we can send them for you and book anyone still interested back on your calendar. First batch is free.
>
> {sender_first}

## A/B arm (all trades, from send 1)

- **Arm A:** as above (the free gift).
- **Arm B:** the same email 1, but the last paragraph becomes *"We send the follow-ups for you and book anyone still interested back on your calendar. You only pay for appointments that get booked. Want to see how it works?"* (risk reversal in place of the gift).

Decision rule: after 500 firms per arm, keep the arm with more positive replies. If neither is clearly ahead, keep the arm with more booked calls.

## When someone says yes to the gift
Within 2 business hours:
1. Write the 3 emails from their website (company name, services, city). The first is "Are you still planning the {project}?", the second offers a reason to act (spring slots or financing, only if they offer it), and the third is a polite close-the-file.
2. Reply with them, plus one line: *"If it helps, we can send these for you and handle the replies. First batch free. Want a 15-min call to see if it fits?"* Include the booking link.
