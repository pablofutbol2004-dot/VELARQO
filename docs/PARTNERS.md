# Distribution partners (the cheat code)

Written 2026-10-10. Status: **draft, nothing sent, Pablo approves first.**
Why: cold email is one installer at a time. Each company below already sells
to hundreds of installers or roofers. One yes = a warm intro to a whole
network. Source: `docs/PATH_6_MONTHS.md` section 9 and `docs/HARD_TO_FAIL_PLAN.md`.

Full fact-sheets with every source URL:
`docs/knowledge/web_research/2026-10-10_partners_fabricators.md` and
`docs/knowledge/web_research/2026-10-10_partners_vendors_crm_roofing_bodies.md`.
Tags: [V] vendor's own claim, [3P] third party (trade press etc.), [?] not published.
Named people come from dated articles; confirm each on LinkedIn before writing.

## 1. What we offer a partner

- **Fabricators, system houses, trade bodies, CRM vendors:** £100 referral fee
  for every installer who signs with Velarqo through them, paid when that
  installer's first invoice is paid (suggested; Pablo to confirm). Optional:
  we run it under their name ("the [Partner] quote-recovery service").
- **Lead vendors and agencies:** a revenue share on what Velarqo bills their
  clients, instead of £100. The percentage is agreed on the call, never in
  writing before a call (same rule as installer pricing).
- **Never** state the per-survey price or any results, clients or percentages.
  Pre-revenue, zero clients: if asked, say so plainly ("new service, first
  pilots running now").

## 2. The angles

| Partner type | The sentence that matters |
|---|---|
| Fabricator / system house | "Your frames get quoted, then the quote dies in the installer's inbox. You already made the sale to the installer; the installer lost the homeowner. I pick those quotes back up." |
| Lead vendor / agency | "Your leads that went quiet get blamed on you. I chase your clients' quiet quotes and book surveys from them, under your brand if you like, so your retention goes up and you earn a share." |
| CRM / quoting software | "Your users' quote data is sitting there with a status of 'sent'. A partner listing, or a simple export of unsold quotes, turns that into a service your users thank you for." |
| Trade body / merchant | "A member benefit that costs the member nothing unless a survey gets booked. You add it to the benefits menu; I pay a referral fee per member who signs." |

## 4. Referral emails (DRAFTS, not sent, Pablo approves first)

Send rules for partner email (different from installer cold email):
- From Pablo's own velarqo.com mailbox, by hand, one at a time. Never from the
  16 outreach mailboxes or the send engine.
- To a named role at a Ltd company (corporate subscriber under PECR).
  Sender identity and opt-out on every email.
- Plain text, no links in the first touch, no attachment. The referral fee IS
  stated (it is the partner's money, not the installer's price). The
  per-survey price is NOT stated.
- Personalise only from a fact on their website (scheme name, product). If you
  have no fact, use the generic line.

### Email A: fabricators, system houses, CRM/quoting software

```
Subject: your installers' unsold quotes

Hi {first name},

Your installers quote a lot of jobs that go quiet after the frames are priced. {Personalised line, e.g. "Your {scheme name} already helps them sell; this fits beside it."}

I run Velarqo. We chase an installer's old quotes, 3 to 24 months back, by phone and message, and book the ones still interested in for a survey. The installer only pays for surveys that get booked.

I'd like to offer it to your installer network, under your name if you prefer. I pay you £100 for each installer who signs up through you.

Worth a 15-minute call?

Pablo
Velarqo, velarqo.com
Reply "no" and I won't email again.
```

Generic version of the personalised line: "I don't think anyone picks those quotes up again."
For CRM vendors swap "installer network" for "users" and add: "or just a partner listing, if that's simpler."

### Email B: lead vendors and installer-facing agencies

```
Subject: your clients' leads that went quiet

Hi {first name},

Your clients buy leads from you, quote them, then a good share go quiet. Those get blamed on the leads.

I run Velarqo. We chase an installer's quiet quotes, 3 to 24 months old, by phone and message, and book the live ones in for a survey. The installer only pays for surveys that get booked.

I'd like to run it for your clients, under your brand or mine, with a share of the revenue to you. It turns their dead leads into surveys and should help you keep them.

Worth a 15-minute call?

Pablo
Velarqo, velarqo.com
Reply "no" and I won't email again.
```

### Follow-up (day 5, both lists, one only)

Subject: Re: {original subject}

Hi {first name}, one line in case the first got buried. If old quotes aren't something your installers ask about, tell me and I'll leave it there.

## 5. The 30-minutes-a-day routine (from Mon 10 Nov)

Order of attack: easy doors first to learn the pitch (week 1), the big ones
once two or three calls have happened (week 3). Two emails a day, by hand.

|---|---|---|
| 10-14 Nov | Easy doors: CORC, AdminBase, Quickslide, Masterframe, Windowlink, Glazepoint, Leads 2 Trade, GlasGrowth, Home Improvement Growth, OS Group | Mon: 30 min verifying names on LinkedIn and ringing two switchboards for "who looks after your installer network?". Tue-Fri: 2 emails a day. |
| 17-21 Nov | Middle: Emplas, Ultraframe, Permaroof, VELUX, SIG, Burton, Lead Pronto, Quotatis, Certass, NFRC | 2 emails a day + day-5 follow-ups from week 1 + reply to anyone who answered within the hour. |
| 24-28 Nov | Big and slow: VEKA, Rehau, Tommy Trinder, Business Pilot, FENSA/GGF, Eurocell, Liniar, Deceuninck, BMI, Tradify | 2 emails a day, now with what the first calls taught you in the personalised line. |
| Dec | Calls, follow-ups, first referral terms agreed | 30 min: follow-ups, call prep, one-page partner terms for whoever says yes. |

Every day (30 min, after the installer replies are done):
1. 10 min: read partner replies, answer each the same day, book calls.
2. 15 min: two new emails. Open their site, find one real fact for the
   personalised line, send from the velarqo.com mailbox, log it.
3. 5 min: update the tracker (`data/partners.csv`, local, not in git:
   name, role, date sent, follow-up date, reply, call date, outcome, fee owed).
   Add "partners: sent / replies / calls" to the 20:00 scoreboard line.

Rules:
- Pablo approves the two templates before the first email goes out.
- Nothing goes to Sternfenster until Velarqo has a result to show.
- One follow-up only. A "no" goes on the suppression list like any other.
- A partner call needs three things ready: how the referral is tracked
  (installer names the partner when signing), when the fee is paid (first
  invoice paid), and the honest answer to "how many clients do you have?".
- Stop the routine early if a fit-5 partner says yes: a signed partner deal
  is worth more than finishing the list.
