## 2. What we do

We follow up your old quotes that never turned into jobs, by text and email
in your business's name, using the messages you approve. Homeowners who are
still interested get booked into your diary for a survey. We do not sell,
quote prices, offer discounts or make promises on your behalf beyond the
approved wording.

## 3. Which quotes are used

A quote is **eligible** only if all of these are true: it is 3 to 24 months
old; it was not won; the homeowner hasn't opted out and isn't on your
do-not-contact list; it has a phone number or email; it is inside your
service area ([postcodes]); and you collected it yourself (see `06`).

We may leave out any record whose source is unclear. You can't ask us to
message anyone who has opted out.

**Comparison group (holdout).** About [15]% of eligible quotes (between 10%
and 20%) are picked at random and not contacted by us during the pilot. You
agree to this. It lets us both see what the follow-up added compared with
doing nothing. Please tell us if you run your own campaign to these old
quotes during the pilot, as it affects the comparison.

## 4. What counts as a booked survey

A **booked survey** is a homeowner who, from an eligible quote and in reply
to our messages, confirms a specific date and time for a survey in your
calendar, for a product you offer, at an address in your area. It is
recorded in the booking calendar and the message log.

It is **not** a booked survey if:
- the homeowner only says "send me info" or "maybe" with no date agreed;
- you show, within [7] days of us notifying you of the booking, a dated
  record that you were already in contact with that homeowner about this job
  in the [90] days before our first message to them (an active lead);
- it is outside your area or for something you don't do;
- the homeowner is in the comparison group (we never contacted them);
- the person is the wrong person, or the booking was made without the
  homeowner clearly agreeing.

**Edge cases:**
- **Rebooks.** A booking that's moved or rebooked is still one booked survey.
- **Same household.** One charge per address per pilot, even if two people
  or two old quotes at that address reply.
- **Homeowner contacts you directly.** If a homeowner we messaged books a
  survey with you directly (phone, website) within [14] days of our last
  message to them, it counts as a booked survey and you tell us. After
  [14] days it doesn't count.
- **Cancellations.** If the homeowner cancels and isn't rebooked within [14]
  days, it's treated as a no-show (credited).

## 6. What you do

- Approve every message word for word before it is sent, using the approval
  sheet (`09`). Any change to the wording needs a new approval.
- Only approve offers you'll honour (for example a free re-survey or an
  updated price).
- Confirm how the quotes were collected and send your do-not-contact list
  before launch, and tell us of any new opt-outs straight away.
- Keep the survey slots you give us available, and attend or cover every
  booked survey.
- Answer anything we pass to you (complaints, pricing questions) within
  [4] working hours.
- Record the outcome of each survey (attended, no-show, requoted, won, job
  value) within [2] working days.

## 7. What we do

- Only contact eligible homeowners, only with approved messages, and stop
  for good when anyone opts out.
- Answer homeowner replies within 1 working hour during [hours, days].
- Pass complaints, pricing disputes, upset or vulnerable people and any data
  request to you the same working day.
- Send you a weekly report (`10`) and keep a full log of messages and
  bookings you can ask to see.
- Keep the data safe and use it only for this pilot (see `05`).

## 8. Pausing and stopping

We pause the pilot (no new homeowners contacted) if any of these happen.
Messages already sent finish their current step.

| Signal | Pause if |
|---|---|
| Opt-out/STOP rate | > 8% of contacted homeowners in a batch |
| Complaints (angry reply, "how did you get my number", mention of ICO) | 2 or more in a batch, or any mention of the ICO/a regulator |
| Text delivery failures | > 10% of a batch |
| Wrong-person replies | > 5% of a batch |
| You can't cover booked surveys | any survey unattended because of you |
| Our manual time | > 60 min per 100 homeowners contacted |
| Replies lost or not answered within 1 working hour | any |

We restart only after we've both agreed what changed. These limits can't be
loosened during the pilot.

Either of us may also pause at once if there's a legal or reputation risk.

## 9. How long it lasts and how it ends

The pilot runs [6] weeks from the first message, or until the cap is
reached, whichever is first. Either of us can end it early with [7] days'
notice by email, or at once if the other seriously breaks this agreement.
On ending: no new messages go out, booked surveys already in the diary
stay, the final invoice covers surveys booked up to the end date less
credits, and data is deleted or returned within 30 days (see `05`). We keep
only totals (counts) that don't identify anyone.

## 10. Data and ownership

You own your quote data and are responsible for it (the controller). We
process it only on your instructions (the processor), under `05`. Our
message templates, workflows and software stay ours; you can keep copies of
the messages you approved.

by law. We don't promise any number of replies, bookings or sales.
