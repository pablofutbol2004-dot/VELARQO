# Getting started: intake form

For [installer]. Takes about 15 minutes. Short answers are fine. If you're
not sure, write "not sure" and we'll sort it on a call.
The right-hand column says what each answer is used for.

## 1. Your business

| Question | Your answer | What we use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Legal company name and company number | | The pilot terms and data agreement |
| Name homeowners know you by (trading name) | | Every message, so they recognise you |
| Town / area you're based in | | Answering "who is this?" |

## 2. The messages

| Question | Your answer | What we use it for |
|---|---|---|

## 4. Where the old quotes came from

This decides which quotes we're allowed to message, so please be as exact as
you can (see the notes at the end).

| Question | Your answer | What we use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Where are your quotes kept (spreadsheet, CRM, quoting software)? | | Getting a clean export |
| How did homeowners ask for a quote? Roughly what share each: website form / phone / email / showroom / Checkatrade, Bark or other lead sites / bought leads / other | | Only quotes you collected yourself can be messaged |
| Please attach a screenshot of your website quote form, including any small print | | Proof of what people were told |
| When you took their details, were they told they could opt out of hearing from you again? How (form wording, on the phone, privacy notice)? | | Needed for the legal basis ("soft opt-in") |
| Link to your privacy notice | | Checking it covers following up quotes |
| Is any of the data from another company (old business, group company, partner, list you bought)? | | That data can't be used |
| Do you keep a do-not-contact / opt-out list? Please send it (phone and email columns are enough) | | Those people are never contacted |
| Any quotes your team is actively chasing right now? | | Left out, so we don't chase your live leads |

## 5. Confirmation

I confirm the answers above are accurate to the best of my knowledge, that
the quote data we send was collected by [company] directly from the
homeowners, and that I'll tell Velarqo straight away about any new opt-outs.

Name: ______________ Role: ______________ Date: ______

---

Notes on section 4: the law on marketing texts and emails (PECR) only lets a
business message people who asked it for a quote if it collected their
details itself, gave them a chance to opt out at the time, and gives one in
every message. Leads passed on by lead sites, or bought, usually don't
qualify. That's why we ask.
