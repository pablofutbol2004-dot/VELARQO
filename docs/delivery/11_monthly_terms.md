# Monthly terms: every new quote chased

Two things live here: **A**, the one-page sheet sent to an installer after
the pilot results call (never before a call, never cold); and **B**, the
continuation clause to add to the pilot agreement (`04`). Prices are
anchors under test (`docs/PRICING.md`, "The monthly product"); fill them in
from what was said on the call, never from this file by default.

House rules apply: plain English, British spelling, no results claims, no
percentages, nothing about other clients. Counts and prices only.

---

## A. The sheet (one page, sent as PDF after the call)

**Velarqo: monthly follow-up for [Installer]**
Sent [date] after our call on [date]. Valid for 30 days.

### What you get

**Chase** (£[149] a month + £[35] per booked survey)

- Every new quote you send us gets followed up by text and email in your
  name, over about 30 days, with the wording you've already approved.
- A person (Pablo) answers every reply within one working hour, 9am-7pm,
  Monday to Saturday. Nothing goes out on Sundays or after 7pm.
- Homeowners who are still interested get booked straight into your survey
  diary, with a confirmation and a reminder text.
- When you tell us a job is finished, we ask the customer for a review.
- A one-page report each month, and the booking log whenever you want it.

**Chase + Answer** (£[349] a month + £[20] per booked survey)

Everything in Chase, plus:

- **Missed calls get a text back** within a minute, from a UK number, and
  a person follows up. You keep your own number: unanswered calls are
  diverted to ours. (If you'd rather, we can set the number up for you to
  use on adverts instead.)
- **Every new enquiry gets an instant first reply** (web form, email or
  text) within 5 minutes in working hours, and a person within the hour.
- **Out of hours**, an automatic reply says when someone will call back and
  asks the two or three things a surveyor needs to know.
- **Once a quarter** we re-chase the quotes that have gone three months
  quiet, the same way as the pilot.

### How the per-survey fee works

- A **booked survey** means the same as in the pilot: a homeowner from
  your quotes (or, on Chase + Answer, a caller or enquiry we answered)
  who confirms a date and time for a survey, for something you do, in
  your area. It's written in the booking log you can see.
- **If the homeowner isn't in** when you turn up, or you tried to reach
  them and couldn't, that survey is credited on the next invoice. If it's
  missed because of you (cancelled, didn't turn up, wrong address given),
  it's still charged.
- A moved or rebooked survey is one survey. One charge per homeowner in
  any 90 days. A cancellation not rebooked within 14 days counts as a
  no-show and is credited.
- **Weekly cap:** you tell us how many surveys you can attend in a week
  ([5] on Chase, [8] on Chase + Answer, or whatever you set). Once we hit
  it, we offer the homeowner dates the following week instead. Raise it
  any time by email.
- **Pay per attended survey instead?** If you'd rather only pay when the
  homeowner is in, the fee is £[50] on Chase / £[30] on Chase + Answer,
  and you record attended or no-show within two working days. A survey
  with nothing recorded after seven days is treated as attended.

### What you do

- Send us each new quote the day you send it to the homeowner (forward
  the email, or a weekly export: we'll set up whichever is easier).
- Keep the survey slots you give us available and attend every booked
  survey.
- Tell us the outcome of each survey (attended, no-show, requoted, won)
  within two working days, and tell us when a job is finished.
- Only approve offers you'll honour. Tell us about any new opt-outs
  straight away.

### Money

- The monthly fee is invoiced in advance on the 1st (the first month pro
  rata from your start date). Per-survey fees for the previous month go on
  the same invoice, less any credits. Payment within 14 days by bank
  transfer. [VAT wording to confirm.]
- Query any line within 5 working days; we settle it from the booking log
  and the message record.
- No setup fee if you start within 30 days of the pilot ending.

### Starting and stopping

- Live within 5 working days of you signing this sheet. The messages you
  approved in the pilot carry over.
- Either of us can end it with **30 days' notice by email**. Surveys
  already in the diary stay and are invoiced. Follow-ups already running
  finish or stop, as you prefer. On Chase + Answer, the call diversion is
  removed on the end date (the number stays with us). Your data is
  returned or deleted within 30 days, as in the data processing agreement.

### What we don't promise

We don't promise a number of replies, bookings or jobs. What you see in
the pilot report is what happened for you; we can't say it will repeat.
You pay the monthly fee for the follow-up being run, and the per-survey
fee only for surveys that get booked.

## B. Continuation clause for the pilot agreement (`04`)

Add as **section 9A** of `04_pilot_agreement.md`, after "How long it lasts
and how it ends". Marked for the UK lawyer, together with the rest of `04`.

> **9A. After the pilot**
>
> Nothing continues automatically. When the pilot ends we send you the
> results and, if you want it, a one-page monthly terms sheet. That sheet
> covers follow-up on your new quotes as they come in (and, if you choose,
> answering missed calls and enquiries), for a monthly fee plus a smaller
> fee per booked survey, ending on 30 days' notice by either of us.
>
> If you sign the sheet within 30 days of the pilot ending: there is no
> setup fee; the messages you approved for the pilot carry over; and
> sections 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12 of this agreement, and the data
> processing agreement, continue to apply unless the sheet says otherwise.
> Where the sheet and this agreement differ on price, cap or notice, the
> sheet wins.
>
> If you don't sign, section 9 applies as written and nothing more is
> owed.
