## 2. Our duties

We will:

1. **Instructions.** Process the data only on your documented instructions:
   this agreement, the pilot agreement, the approved messages and anything
   else you tell us in writing (email is fine). That includes any transfer
   outside the UK (section 4). If we think an instruction breaks data
   protection law, we tell you and don't act on it.
2. **Confidentiality.** Only Pablo has access. If anyone else ever helps, they
   are bound by confidentiality in writing first.
3. **Security.** Keep the data safe, including: [encrypted disk on the
   computer and server holding the database]; strong unique passwords and
   two-factor sign-in on every account; the export files kept only in a
   client folder outside any shared or public location; no data in email
   beyond what's needed; access removed when the pilot ends. [To confirm
   with Pablo: where the database actually runs during the pilot.]
4. **Sub-processors.** Only use the sub-processors listed in section 3. To add
   or change one, we tell you [14] days ahead so you can object; if you
   object and we can't avoid it, either of us may end the pilot. Each
   sub-processor is bound by data protection terms at least as protective as
   these, and we remain responsible to you for them.
5. **Rights requests.** If a homeowner asks to see, correct or delete their
   data, or objects, we pass it to you within [2] working days and help you
   answer. An opt-out (STOP, "don't contact me") we act on at once ourselves
   and tell you.
6. **Breaches.** Tell you without undue delay, and in any case within
   [24] hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with what we know
   and what we're doing. This lets you meet the 72-hour deadline to tell the
   ICO where needed.
7. **Other help.** Help you with security, breach reports and any data
   protection impact assessment, as far as it relates to our processing.
8. **End of pilot.** Within 30 days of the end, delete the personal data
   (including from the sending platform sub-account) or, if you ask before
   then, return it to you first. We may keep only counts that don't identify
   anyone, and the minimum record of opt-outs if you instruct us to keep it
   so those people are never contacted again. We confirm deletion in writing.
9. **Audits.** Give you the information you need to show we meet these
   terms, and allow a reasonable audit or inspection by you or someone you
   appoint, with [14] days' notice, at your cost, no more than once a year
   unless there's been a breach.
10. **Records.** Keep a record of the processing we do for you.
