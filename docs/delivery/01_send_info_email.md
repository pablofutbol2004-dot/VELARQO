# Reply when an installer says "send me info"

Use as a reply in the same thread. Plain text, no attachment. Fill the
`[...]` bits; `{company}` is their company name. Never state a price in
writing (prices are tested on calls, see `docs/PRICING.md`).

---

Hi [name],

Short version of how it works:

1. You send me a list of old quotes that never turned into jobs. A sample of
   20-50 is enough to start, and names and numbers can be blanked out.
2. I check which ones are worth chasing and tell you what I find. No cost.
3. If it looks good, you approve the exact messages first. Then I follow
   those homeowners up in {company}'s name, by text and email.
4. Anyone still interested gets booked into your diary for a survey.

You only pay for surveys that get booked, and a survey that doesn't happen
because the homeowner doesn't turn up isn't charged. Anyone who says no is
never contacted again.

I also leave a small slice of the old quotes alone, so you can see what the
follow-up added compared with doing nothing.

Roughly how many quotes from the last 1-2 years never turned into a job?

Pablo
Velarqo, velarqo.com
