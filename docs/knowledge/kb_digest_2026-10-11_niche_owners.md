## 2. Existing Velarqo docs: owner and niche facts only

- **Pricing anchors the owner already pays** (`web_research/2026-10-10_competitors.md`, UK, mostly vendor pages): shared fresh lead £15-120 (roofing mid ~£55); booked appointment from £130; cost per won job from shared leads ~£275-550; CRM with chasing £37-44 per user (Tradify), £240+/month (Business Pilot); Checkatrade £30-500/month. A booked survey at or below ~£130 is at the going rate.
- **They are over-pitched and sceptical.** Installer-facing ad agencies all use "pay on results, money back, named revenue" copy; Lead Pronto has 25% one-star Trustpilot reviews for dead leads and refunds (competitors file, UK, 3P/vendor).
- **Quote chasing is already sold at the system-house level.** Sternfenster EasyAdmin+ emails at 3, 7 and 14 days (UK, vendor claim "up to 36%"); Glasgrowth advertises "start with the old quotes in your phone" (`competitors.md`, `partners_vendors_crm_roofing_bodies.md`).
- **The tools they use** (`partners_vendors_crm_roofing_bodies.md`, UK, vendor claims): Tommy Trinder/Framepoint "702 window companies", Business Pilot ("thousands", real count unknown), AdminBase "hundreds", Glazepoint tiny. Roofers: no specialist UK tool found.
- **Reach of trade bodies:** FENSA "6,500 to 9,000" members depending on page, NFRC ~1,200-1,350 and skewed to larger contractors, Certass price-led from £24.95/month. About 12,000 UK window-installation firms and ~12,000 roofing businesses (`HARD_TO_FAIL_PLAN.md`, Insight Data and IBISWorld, 3P).
- **What a call asks** (`CALL_SCRIPT.md`, `SALES_PROCESS.md`): quotes per month, how many convert, what a job is worth, who chases and when they stop, where quotes live (CRM, quoting software, spreadsheet, email), what they pay today for a survey. Nobody has answered yet: `messages`, `replies` tables are empty, one pilot row exists.
- **Chat research (ChatGPT, unverified):** best niches are roofing, windows and doors, HVAC replacement, kitchen/bath, solar; target £500k-£10M revenue, not one-man trades; handyman too low-ticket (`chat_research/2026-10-05_batch2_contractors_ghl_growth_partner.md`). `youtube_research/home_services.md` avoid-list: sole traders, tiny databases, word-of-mouth-only businesses, no exportable data.

## 3. Hard numbers from our own list (VERIFIED)

**Source:** Postgres `velarqo-postgres` (:5437), read-only. `outreach_queue` view (the send list), joined to `companies`, `company_facts` (`ch_officers`, `ch_psc`, `ch_profile`, stored 4-11 Oct) and `contacts`. Counted 11 Oct 2026; ages as at that date.

**List today = 7,512**, not 6,450 (plan figure of 10 Oct): roofing 3,698, windows 2,880, kitchens 934. Kitchens were added since, and windows/roofing grew.

**Bias to remember:** the list is UK **Ltd companies** with an **email found on their own website**. Sole traders, the handyman end, and anyone with no website are absent by construction. Screwfix's 2025 panel says 54% of tradespeople are "just me" (web file, UK, VERIFIED sample of ~700). So everything below describes the more formal, web-visible firms.

### Company age (years since incorporation)
| | <2 | 2-5 | 5-10 | 10-20 | 20+ | Median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Windows (2,844) | 12% | 13% | 21% | 27% | 27% | 11.4 y |
| Roofing (3,698) | **37%** | 20% | 18% | 15% | 11% | **3.7 y** |
| Kitchens (934) | 16% | 19% | 24% | 27% | 15% | 8.1 y |
| All (7,476) | 25% | 17% | 20% | 21% | 17% | 6.7 y |

**Roofing is a young-company list.** Roofing incorporations in the list: 2022: 195, 2023: 262, 2024: 321, **2025: 631, 2026 to date: 656**. Windows stay flat at ~110-170 a year. So 1,287 of 3,698 roofers (35%) were formed since January 2025. 1,308 roofers (35%) have filed no accounts yet, 99% of them under two years old. Why the surge: GUESS (sole traders incorporating, plus possible shell or phoenix companies); I have not checked.

**Established core** (5+ years trading and has filed accounts): 4,335 firms (58%): windows 2,130, roofing 1,593, kitchens 612. 10+ years: 2,848 (38%). These are the firms plausibly holding a backlog of old quotes. Roofing's brand-new companies mostly will not.

### Accounts type (proxy for size)
Micro-entity: windows 36%, roofing 32%, kitchens 41%. "Total exemption full" (small, unaudited): 44% / 28% / 40%. No accounts filed yet: 11% / 35% / 14%. Small, medium, full, group: ~1-2% together. Turnover and headcount are **not stored** (micro filers publish no profit and loss). Micro-entity limits are about £1m turnover and 10 staff since April 2025 (from memory, check before quoting). This fits the web file's finding that average roofing firm size is ~3.4 staff.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Windows (4,670) | **51** | 20% | 41% | 12% |
| Roofing (4,940) | **41** | 45% | 23% | 6% |

All directors by decade: 20s 9%, 30s 22%, 40s 26%, 50s 23%, 60s 15%, 70s 4%, 80s 1%. Oldest director per company: median 54 (windows), 40 (roofing), 50 (kitchens). Among established firms (5+ years, accounts filed): median director age 54 windows, 49 roofing, 52 kitchens.

**Age follows company age.** Windows directors: 41 (company under 2 y), 42, 45, 53, 58 (20+ y). Roofing: 34, 37, 41, 55 (10+ y). Older firms are run by older owners; the new roofing wave is run by men in their 30s. **Founders start at about 36** (median age of directors appointed on incorporation day, n=7,593, middle half 30-44).

Contrast with the web file: Screwfix's trade-wide panel skews 55-64 (34%). Our incorporated list is younger: 32% are 55+. Plausible reason: GUESS, young trades incorporate early; older sole traders never do.

**Gender and nationality** (proxy): from titles on persons with significant control, 17% women (windows 20%, kitchens 22%, roofing 13%). 6% of directors are not British, Irish, English or Scottish by declared nationality. 94% reside in the UK.

### Solo vs partners vs family
Active directors per company: 1: windows 54%, roofing **74%**, kitchens 56%, all **64%**. Two: 35% / 21% / 36% / 28%. Three: 8% / 3% / 5% / 5%. Four or more: 4% / 2% / 3% / 3%.
- Established firms (5+ y): 52% have one director, 36% two, 12% three or more.
- One individual owns 75-100% of the shares (PSC register): windows 46%, roofing 70%, kitchens 52%, all 59%.
- **Family proxy:** among companies with 2+ directors, 56-58% share a surname (excluding common South-Asian surnames makes no difference). That is **20% of all companies** (windows 26%, kitchens 25%, roofing 15%). Treat as a ceiling for "family firm": a shared surname is most likely a couple or father and son, but could be coincidence in common names (Smith is the top surname with 152 directors).
- **Read:** roughly two in three of these firms are one person's business. In windows and kitchens, about a quarter have a family pair at the top. The call script's "who chases, who is in the office" question will often be answered "me" or "my wife".

### Website, email, ads (all from the website, so limited)
- **Website share: not measurable.** Every list firm has one by selection (7,502 of 7,512 emails were scraped from the firm's own site). Among the other ~26,600 right-trade companies without email, only ~3,500 have a website recorded; a name-guessing domain finder tried ~19,300 and found ~100 (0.5%), so a missing website is mostly "not found", not "does not exist".
- **Facebook-only share: not stored.** The website field holds no Facebook URLs (1 of ~36,700 in-trade companies). The batch-6 Grokbot brief asks for this in a note field; nothing loaded yet.
- **Website platform: not stored** (snapshots keep title, text, emails, phones, ad tags, not HTML generators). TLD: 83% `.co.uk`, 15% `.com`, 2% other `.uk`.
- **Email type:** own domain 76% (windows 84%, roofing **67%**, kitchens 88%); Gmail 13% (roofing 18%); Hotmail/Outlook/Live 6%; BT/Sky/TalkTalk-type ISP 1%. **Generic inbox** (info@, office@, sales@): 68% (windows 78%, roofing 60%, kitchens 74%). So most emails reach the office address, which is the owner or his partner in a 1-2 person firm (GUESS).
- **Ad tags** (pixel and badge scan on 3,027 saved pages: windows 2,827, roofing only 200, kitchens none; so roofing and kitchens are mostly unmeasured): windows show a Checkatrade badge 8%, Google Ads tag 8%, Meta pixel 7%, TrustATrader 1%, Bark/MyBuilder/Rated People ~0%; 79% show none. Windows firms running Meta or Google ads: 14%; on a lead marketplace: 9%. Roofing sample of 200: Checkatrade 10%.
- **Accreditation words on their site:** page regex flags accredited (FENSA, Certass, TrustMark, Which? Trusted Trader, Competent Roofer) for windows 28%, roofing 11%, kitchens 3%. Stored accreditation field (undercount): FENSA 330, Checkatrade 293, Certass 109, Which? Trusted Trader 67, TrustMark 52.
- **Site language:** residential wording on 91%; "free survey / home visit" on 10%; explicit "no pressure / no pushy sales" on 7% (windows 12%): the pressure-selling scar the web file describes shows up in how a tenth of windows sites market themselves.

## 4. What this adds to "who is the owner" (labelled)

1. **Windows owner:** a man (80%) in his early 50s, running a firm that is 11 years old (median), with a second director in 46% of cases, who is a spouse or relative in about half of those. Sells and surveys himself or has one rep. VERIFIED for age, tenure, structure; "sells himself" is GUESS.
2. **Roofing owner:** a man (87%) of about 41 who is the only director in three firms out of four, in a company that is under four years old. A third of the list formed in the last 21 months. VERIFIED. This is a tradesperson-turned-owner, not a business-school owner (GUESS, supported by the DPR roofing profile in the web file).
3. **Kitchen firm:** closest to windows (median 49, 44% have 2+ directors, 12% on a free or ISP email), with a bigger share of women (22%) and more showroom-style firms. VERIFIED/GUESS mix.
4. **Handyman vs business:** our list is already the "business" end (incorporated, has a site, 58% established with filed accounts). Inside it, a rough split: ~2,100 established firms with 2+ directors (more business-like), ~2,200 established one-director firms (owner-operator), ~1,900 under two years old (just incorporated; little backlog). GUESS on mindset; counts are VERIFIED (4,335 established, 52% solo / 48% multi; 25% under 2 y).
5. **Don't over-read ages for retirement.** Only 9% of directors are 65+, 12% in windows. The "no successor" angle from the web file (69% have nobody to pass the business to, Screwfix 2024, UK) is a trade-wide figure, not something our list shows.
