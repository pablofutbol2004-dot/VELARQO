## The core conclusion
Faceless content *can* bring B2B leads, but **"faceless" isn't the strategy.** What brings leads is niche, proof-heavy, commercially relevant content connected to a conversion funnel.

There are two different games:
- **Attention:** views, memes, avatars, generic tips.
- **Acquisition:** case studies, teardowns, diagnostics, niche long-form.

The recurring funnel: content → relevant person visits your profile or asset → sees proof → consumes more specific content → CTA, DM or lead magnet → conversation → sale.

## Key rules
- **Don't position on AI.** Talk about the problem (lost enquiries, quote follow-up, dormant databases, speed-to-lead, recovered £); AI is just how you deliver it.
- **Carousel craft:**
  - Slide 1 opens a loop, slide 2 pays it off.
  - Each slide advances one argument (not interchangeable tips), and the visuals change as the argument does.
  - The *idea and offer* produce the lead; the format only packages it (Taki).
- **Micro-magnets beat ebooks.** Something that gives an instant result, e.g. a "Window & Door Dormant Revenue Calculator" or a "5-minute lead leakage audit". It filters for real owners who have a database; a "100 AI prompts" magnet attracts marketers.
- **Faceless is not anonymous.** "Pablo, founder of Velarqo" posting faceless content beats an anonymous @VelarqoAI account.
  - The trust penalty is real (Richard Yu, Sam O'Halloran, Finn Holt). Replace the face with **proof + specificity + transparency + a visible mechanism + consistency + a real, identifiable person**.
  - Never combine anonymous, avatar, AI voice, generic tips and no proof.
- **Never invent case studies.** Before you have clients, use public teardowns, anonymized audits, clearly labeled hypotheticals, documented pilot experiments, and screenshots of the real system.
- **"Organic" success stories hide paid support:** profile ads, retargeting, ManyChat, setters, outbound DMs, email, lead magnets, calls.
- **The Claude content workflow:** references → defined brand voice → templates → generation → self-review → **human correction**. Never "generate 500 posts and walk away".

## Velarqo content machine (built alongside outbound, not before it)
- **3 pillars:**
  1. Money leaks: where window companies lose paid enquiries.
  2. Reactivation mechanics: how dormant-enquiry recovery works.
  3. Proof and experiments: pilots, audits, datasets, outcomes.
  - Nothing about AI news, productivity or entrepreneurship.
- **Weekly cadence:** 2–3 visual posts or carousels, 1 opinionated LinkedIn post, and 1 long-form faceless explainer every 1–2 weeks. Claude repurposes the long-form into the shorter pieces.
- **Funnel:** ICP sees content → Velarqo or Pablo profile → case studies and mechanism → CTA to a **Dormant Lead Revenue Audit** → 3–5 qualifying questions → personalized result → pilot or call.
- **Outbound reuses the same assets:** "Made a 6-minute breakdown of how I'd reactivate a database like yours. Happy to send it."
- **Optimize revenue per unit of attention.** 1,800 views → 70 window people → 4 conversations → 1 pilot beats 380k views with 0 window companies.
- **Identity:** a faceless *specialist research or consulting* brand, not a faceless media brand.
