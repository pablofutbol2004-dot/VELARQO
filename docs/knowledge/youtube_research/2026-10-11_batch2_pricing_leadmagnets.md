## The ideas that matter for Velarqo

1. **With zero proof, risk reversal is the main lever.**
   - **Fazio:** a buyer weighs expected return against risk. Case studies raise the return side. A guarantee or pay-on-results lowers the risk side. If you have no case studies, you have to lower the risk. [cjLYprd-LS4]
   - **Callum:** advertise the pay-per-result option, then offer two choices on the call ("split close"): pay-per-result, deliberately priced higher, or a fixed 3-month fee. Most pick the fixed fee. The ones who pick per-result often pay far more ($7k in a month from one client). [R4QxPab3ePE, kLWwyikPYv8]
   - **Noah:** pay-per-appointment suits trades, where results come fast. It doesn't suit slow-cycle niches like real estate. [qHH0sBJ0Fj8]
2. **The free lead magnet is the biggest lever on replies.** (Hormozi, via Instantly [j5uPj9A7zlE])
   - Give away something others charge for, then turn it into a repeatable process.
   - A lead-gen agency's example: "a free list of 500 verified prospects".
   - It works because buyers already know what that thing costs.
3. **Cold email copy rules** (Hormozi via Instantly; Berman [881Dr4lMey4]):
   - Write at a third-grade reading level. Hormozi says that alone got him 50% more replies.
   - Half a page at most. About 80 words.
   - Use exact numbers, never ranges, and only true ones.
   - Never let it look mass-sent.
   - Subject lines like "quick question".
   - Formulas:
     - Pain + call to action.
     - Pain + partial fix + "want the rest?" (the old "two ideas" email).
     - The competitor's name in the subject line, which opens a loop.
   - Prospects check your **social profile** (LinkedIn) more than your website.
4. **The list comes first** (Hormozi, Floris Jansen [KgnmEoNpeTA]):
   - Define the buyer precisely, build the list for them, then write the copy.
   - Segment by persona.
   - Never send from the main domain.
   - Hold out 10% of the market so you can prove the uplift.
   - Re-send to non-responders after 3-6 months with a new angle.
5. **Distribution through partners who already have the buyers** (Pavlo [sXBDL9bvyhE]):
   - One agency grew through Facebook-group owners in the painting niche.
   - Another worked through dental labs that email their dentist lists, paying the labs 10-15% of sales.
   - This is the fastest shortcut to trust for someone with no proof.
6. **The sales call** (Noah [4iMiPZOlj04], Callum):
   - Discovery covers: current state, what they've tried, "bridges", goal, urgency.
   - "Bridges" means asking "why not just keep doing referrals?" so the prospect states the reason themselves.
   - A two-call close reduced Callum's no-shows.
   - Call new leads within 5 minutes.
   - Record and review your calls.
7. **Sequence:** outbound until about $4-5k a month, then ads. Sales skill comes from reps, and cold outreach is how you get them (Pavlo, Callum, Hormozi).
8. **Pick a sub-niche inside the niche** (Noah): for example, cleaning firms of a certain size that clean offices. Messaging gets sharper, and so do results.
9. **Productised services** (Greg Hickman [UQEn-ku1oYU]):
   - Expect churn around months 3-6.
   - Cheap clients churn fastest.
   - Treat price as something you keep tuning.
   - You still need a sales call.
10. **Later-stage lessons** (Alisha [6iuSnzI6yRU], Instantly [hZi1-OvVdck], Fazio):
    - Own the outcome, not the tasks.
    - Find the one bottleneck and fix it.
    - Retain clients by keeping their goals and yours aligned, with weekly communication.
    - Build operating systems: SOPs, onboarding, hiring.

## Addendum: 2 more videos (same day)

- **Jordan Platten, channels ranked by cost and speed [SgeJoDWc_9U]:**
  - **Cold email:** about 0.5% of emails become a meeting (roughly 200 emails per meeting), but it moves a lot with the offer and the list. Slow to start (warm-up, then follow-ups).
  - **Cold calling:** about 4% of dials become a meeting, with money that week.
  - **Physical mail to dream clients:** about 10% respond and about 5% book. He calls it "unfair" because almost nobody does it.
  - **Door to door:** 6-10% book. Not possible for Pablo.
  - **Partner referrals:** top tier.
  - **Ads:** "no proof, no ads".
  - **Content:** slowest to start but compounds.
  - Give any channel 90 days.
- **Adam Erhart, build it first and give it away [EJ8sQIBEwRk]:**
  - Do the work before asking, and frame it as a gift: "I built you one, want to see it?" rather than "want me to build you one?"
  - Use a real 7-day preview deadline.
  - Charge for keeping the system running ($297 a month).
  - Caution: he earns affiliate fees from GoHighLevel, and the website offer itself is a crowded, cheap commodity.

**Applied:**
- The free prospect list from hook B gets **pre-built for each firm before the email goes out**, so the line "I put together 50 businesses near you that fit your size" is true when sent.
