# Monthly product anchors + renewables niche verdict (web research, 2026-10-10)

Tags: P primary, S secondary, V vendor. Fetched pages where possible; "unverified" = from search summary only.

## A. Monthly product

### What trades software already bundles (UK, ex-VAT)
- Powered Now (P, powerednow.com/pricing): Business £28, Professional £32/user, Premium £40/user; only Premium has automatic quote chasing, appointment and invoice reminders. "PN Receptionist" add-on £29-39/month.
- Jobber (P, USD only): Core $29, Connect $99 (quote/invoice follow-up automation starts here), Grow $149, Plus $399 (AI receptionist included only in Plus); Receptionist add-on $29.
- Tradify ~£34/user (S); ServiceM8 £25 flat (S); Sayora ~£49/month UK number (V); Business Pilot/Glazepoint: nothing found.
- GoHighLevel: Starter $97 (3 sub-accounts), Unlimited $297 (rebilling), Agency Pro $497 (SaaS mode); SMS/calls/AI metered on top (S/V).
- Meaning: a tradesperson can buy a quote-chase sequence for ~£40/user. Velarqo competes on someone *running* it and booking surveys, not on the sequence.

### Published UK prices, missed-call text-back / receptionist / follow-up (all V)
Swift Site £39.99+£49 setup; Call2SMS £37/67/97; Hand On Web £49/99/149; Call Catchers £35; TextBack Pro from £47; 1nexus £77; Kopa "Growth Engine" £197 (site + reviews + follow-up + local SEO); Softomate AI receptionist £99-149 basic, £150-299 with booking, £299-500 with outbound follow-up. Generic UK agency retainers £999-3,500 (S). No UK agency found selling "speed-to-lead" as a priced installer retainer. Pure text-back is a £35-100 commodity; above ~£200 must include booked outcomes.

### Evidence on speed / follow-up
- "5 minutes" claim: 2007 MarketingSherpa presentation (Elkington/InsideSales + Oldroyd); odds of contact 100x and qualifying 21x at 5 vs 30 min. Vendor data, not peer-reviewed, US B2B web leads, odds ratios not probabilities (V). HBR 2011 "Short Life of Online Sales Leads" is separate (2,241 US firms; 37% replied within an hour, 23% never; S, unverified figures).
- No UK data on quote follow-up lift in trades (Jobber/Tradify/Checkatrade). Don't promise a percentage; measure in the pilot.

### Recommended 2 tiers (agent's judgement, anchors only)
- Tier 1 "Chase": £149/month + £35 per booked survey. Every new quote gets a 5-7 touch SMS/email sequence over ~30 days; review request after completion; monthly "who's open" report. Above DIY (~£40), below Kopa (£197).
- Tier 2 "Chase + Answer": £349/month + £20 per booked survey. Adds missed-call text-back on a UK number, instant reply to every new enquiry (<5 min in hours), out-of-hours text qualification into the survey calendar, quarterly reactivation of the old-quote list. Below the £500 AI-receptionist top.
- GHL needs (unverified here): one sub-account per client from a snapshot; Unlimited ($297) minimum for rebilling; UK LC-Phone number with SMS + forwarding; STOP/quiet hours; quote intake route (CSV/Zapier/webhook/inbox parser); booking link per client; per-client usage cap; booked-survey definition + log for defensible invoices.

## B. UK renewables installers (solar / battery / heat pump)
- Count: MCS search shows 5,647 (P); ~77% solar PV, 58% heat pump, 34% battery certified (S, old).
- **MCS data: effectively off-limits.** Terms (P, mcscertified.com/terms-conditions) prohibit commercial use/resale of content and data. Build lists from Companies House + websites + Google instead.
- Job values: ~£7,600 for a 4.5 kW solar system (V/S); nothing sourced for solar+battery or ASHP.
- Hooks with dates: 0% VAT on solar/batteries/heat pumps ends 31 Mar 2027 (S, check HMRC 708/6); ECO4 ends 31 Dec 2026, no successor (S); BUS £7,500 runs to 2030 (P-ish, MCS Jan 2026); £2,500 air-to-air grant from 18 Nov 2025, heat-battery grant 2026/27 (S); Warm Homes Plan (Jan 2026) £15bn incl. solar loans; council Solar Together windows (London: 72,330 registrations → 3,231 installs: many sign-ups never convert).
- Lead prices (V, Whito Sep 2026): solar leads £8-15 + retainer; Lead Pronto £25/£35, £130 booked appointment; HelloLeads £35-75; MyBuilder from £7; implied £125-600 lead spend per won job.
- No UK data on quote-to-install conversion or lead sources. Marketplace-sourced lists are a weaker soft-opt-in case; get legal confirmation before messaging them.
- **Verdict: NO-GO as niche #2 now** (small pool, MCS list unusable, no conversion data, lumpy grant-driven quotes). Revisit as a Q1 2027 seasonal campaign with a hand-built list. Prefer kitchens, bathrooms, roofing next.
