# UK competitor teardown (web research, 2026-10-10)

[F] fetched from vendor page; [3P] third party / trade press; vendor claims unverified.

## Direct competitors
- **doubleglazingleads.co.uk (LeadCentre Pro)** [F]: Meta/Google ads + UK appointment setters; quote chasing sold only as an add-on. No prices, no guarantee ("cannot guarantee the size or margin of every job"). Windows/doors/conservatories. Claims 6,000+ appointments, 60+ companies. Trustpilot 0 reviews. Gap: quote chasing is a bolt-on to an ad retainer.
- **databasereactivation.co.uk (ProfitWize, Nottingham)** [F]: done-for-you AI SMS reactivation from a CRM export; horizontal (home improvement, dental, aesthetics, finance, automotive). No prices/guarantee; "12 appointments" is a mockup; 98% open / 45% response unsourced. Referral partner programme (rev share). Gap: generic, SMS-only, no installer language, no PECR explanation.
- **Honeybee Business Solutions** [3P only, Construction News Oct 2025]: AI SMS reactivation; double glazing, solar, conservatories; 2-week "no-risk" trial then retainer; no prices, no site found.
- **Leads 2 Trade** [3P, Glass Times]: qualified leads shared with at most one other installer; "no pitch no fee" appointments; leads from £15; claims 2m leads, 65% as confirmed appointments, one installer £16.3k spend → £188k (9% cost of sale). Trustpilot 3.0 (5). FENSA partnership. Gap: fresh leads only.
- **Lead Pronto** [F]: per lead from £15; booked appointment from £130; no contract; 48 sectors incl. windows, roofing, solar; claims £56m ad spend, 3m+ leads, 100+ clients, 82% attended; Anglian case study. Trustpilot 3.7 (39), 25% 1-star (dead leads, refunds). Best-funded rival. Gap: fresh-lead/ad business, nothing on the installer's own database.
- **Glasgrowth** [Meta Ad Library, live Oct 2026, V]: "10-15 qualified homeowner appointments a month. No retainer, we earn on the orders you sign." Also: "Start with the old quotes already sitting in your phone, so appointments can land before the ads even run." WhatsApp reply in 2 minutes, postcode lock. **Closest to Velarqo's old-quote angle and already advertising it.**
- Other installer-facing ad agencies live in the Ad Library (all V): OS Group "Windows on Steroids" (no retainer until first deal), Koobr (100+ enquiries in 30 days), Home Improvement Growth (45+ booked appointments in 90 days or money back), Lazro Growth (£60k in 60 days), WindowCAD. Pattern: pay-on-results, money-back, named revenue numbers, "agencies burned you before" copy. Installers are over-pitched and sceptical.
- **ASN Activate**: not found.

## Lead marketplaces [3P, Local Ladder 30 Sep 2026; Whito]
MyBuilder from £7 (typ. £5-35; roofing £15-60). Bark £1.80/credit, ~£9-36+/lead. Checkatrade from £30/month, lead plans from £59/month on 12-month terms; members report £80-500/month. Rated People reportedly liquidated 16 Sep 2026, brand sold to Checkatrade (single source, verify). Shared roofing leads ~£25-120 (mid £55), 15-25% conversion → ~£275-550 per won job.

## Software with quote follow-up [F unless noted]
Tradify £34/37/44 per user (auto-chasing from £37 Pro). Powered Now £28/32/40 (chasing only on £40 Premium). Jobber $29/99/149/399 (follow-ups from $99). Glazepoint £100 first user + £50/user, no follow-up feature. Business Pilot min £240/month, 24-month term [3P]. Simpro ~£100-150/user + £2-10k setup (low confidence). Sayora not found. **Sternfenster EasyAdmin+** [3P, Glass Times Jan 2026]: auto-emails at 3/7/14 days after a quote, free until Mar 2026 then paid, claims "up to 36%" better conversion (vendor). Gap: all send templated reminders; none gets a conversation to a booked survey.

## Agencies selling quote follow-up to installers
Searched "quote follow-up / chasing / lost quote / estimate follow-up / unsold quotes / old quotes": no UK agency found doing this as its main offer for installers or roofers (Glasgrowth uses it as an opener for ads). Niche appears nearly unoccupied (limited search).

## Positioning gap
1. Everyone sells fresh leads or a retainer. Nobody sells recovery of quotes already paid for, priced per booked survey, as the whole offer.
2. No one publishes a price, a guarantee, or named installer proof. Velarqo can state all three once there is proof.
3. Software chases with templates. Velarqo gets a real reply and the survey in the diary.

## Partner / reseller questions (asked openly, saying who you are)
1. "We recover old quotes for installers, pay-per-booked-survey. Do your clients ask for that? Would a referral arrangement make sense?"
2. "How many of your clients sit on stale quote databases, and what do you do with them today?"
3. "Can your software export quotes with status and date? API or partner programme?"
4. "What do your installers say about quote follow-up? Where does automated chasing fall short?"
5. "What would a fair referral fee or revenue share look like for you?"
