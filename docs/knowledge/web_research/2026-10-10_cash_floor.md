## Invoicing in Spain (S: Quipu, Consejo de Gestores, Billeo, Infoautónomos)
- 036/037 census registration before any invoice; RETA is separate.
- RETA test is habitualidad, not amount: STS 941/2025 (10 Jul 2025) says under-SMI income does not exempt. Monthly invoicing = habitual. Fines €300-3,000 + back contributions +20%.
- Route: RETA with tarifa plana ≈ €80-88.64/month for 12 months (+12 more if net income < SMI ~€17,094); then income-based from ~€200/month. Reduced IRPF withholding 7% (vs 15%) for the first 3 years on invoices to businesses.
- Cooperativas de facturación (Factoo, Cooperativa Online): avoid; disqualification proceedings 2017, no 2026 update found.
- Ask the gestor: IAE epígrafe (763 for programmers?), IVA on Upwork/foreign clients, IVA exemption for private classes, Castilla y León aid for new autónomos.
