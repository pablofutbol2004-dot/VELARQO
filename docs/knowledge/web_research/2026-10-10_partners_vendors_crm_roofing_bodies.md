Researched 2026-10-10, read-only (web search + page fetches; nobody contacted). Tags: [V] = vendor/own-site claim, [3P] = third party, [?] = unknown. Search/fetch summaries were machine-condensed, so every figure below should be re-checked on the linked page before it goes into an email. "Undated" = page carries no date. - "OS Group / Windows on Steroids" and "Home Improvement Growth": no trace found in two searches each. Rows say so rather than guessing.

- First Degree Systems has been absorbed into Cyncly (firstdegreesystems.com redirects to cyncly.com).
- Marley states it runs NO approved-installer scheme.
