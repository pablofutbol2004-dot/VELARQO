# UK commercial service firms: economics, competitors, law (web research, 11 Oct 2026)

Sonnet research agent, checked 11 Oct 2026. Labels: VERIFIED (official or primary source), VENDOR (sells into this market), ANECDOTAL (one person or forum). Several figures came through a page summariser, so re-read a source before quoting it to a client.

## Commercial cleaning economics
- **Small office contract (2,000 sq ft, 5 visits a week): £680-850 a month, about £8-10k a year.** Schools pay £1,400-1,800 a month, and London/South East adds 15-25%. VENDOR, Trade2Base, 27 May 2026, https://www.trade2base.com/blog/commercial-cleaning-contracts-guide
- **"Typical contract worth around £5,000 a year."** ANECDOTAL, from an ex-owner of a £22m cleaning business, 15 Aug 2023, https://thegrowthlabfm.substack.com/p/operational-insight-the-service-proposal
- **Public-sector notices run from about £15k to £117k a year, on 3-year terms.** These are individual notices, not an average. VERIFIED notices, e.g. https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/068725-2025
- **Win rate: one ex-owner converted about 25% of 250+ bids over 8 years.** ANECDOTAL, same Substack. No survey-to-contract figure was found anywhere.
- **Contract length:** housing associations usually run 2-3 years (VENDOR). No figure found for private offices.
- **How firms win work:**
  - Big contracts are often decided on paperwork (CHAS, Constructionline, insurance) before the first meeting. VENDOR, Whito 2026.
  - Owner forums say: start with people you know, then referrals, then phone to find the decision-maker and follow up with a letter or email. ANECDOTAL, UK Business Forums.

## Competitors
- **UK cleaning-specific lead gen is thin.**
  - PromoSEO sells exclusive office-cleaning enquiries "from £500 per month". VENDOR.
  - Pearl Lemon / payperleadservicesagency.com charges £45-160 per lead, or "from $35 per pre-booked appointment". VENDOR.
  - Whito says 8 of 11 agencies selling to cleaners hide their prices. VENDOR.
- **Fire/security installers:** no UK specialist agency found.
- **Nobody sells quote or old-proposal follow-up as a service** to UK B2B contractors. Only software does it (ServiceM8 auto revival, QuotePilot, QuoteMe). "Not found" means no evidence found, not proof that none exists.
- **UK B2B appointment setting in general:**
  - £150-600 per appointment, or retainers of £1,800-12,000 a month.
  - Show rates are 70-80%.
  - These firms are calling-based.
  - VENDOR, The Lead Gen Company, 19 Aug 2026.
- **US janitorial is crowded:**
  - Janitorial Appointment charges $130 per appointment ($910 a month for 7).
  - Abstrakt quotes $40-125 per appointment.
  - Many others exist.
  - IBISWorld counts 1.26m US janitorial businesses in 2026. VENDOR.

## Law (UK PECR, ICO)
- "You can email or text any corporate body (a company, Scottish partnership, limited liability partnership or government body)." VERIFIED, ICO electronic mail marketing guide.
- "Sole traders and some partnerships are treated as individuals." VERIFIED, same page.
- **B2B page:** no consent is needed for corporate subscribers, but you must not hide your identity and must give a valid opt-out. Uncertain addresses should be treated as individuals, and UK GDPR still applies to named employees. VERIFIED in substance (via summariser): https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/
- The ICO guide is "under review" after the Data (Use and Access) Act.
