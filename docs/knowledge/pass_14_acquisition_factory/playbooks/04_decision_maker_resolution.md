# 04 — Decision-maker resolution

## Role order for early-stage Velarqo

1. owner / founder / managing director
2. sales/commercial director or manager
3. general manager
4. marketing/operations leader only when clearly responsible for lead follow-up
5. generic business inbox if a named person cannot be responsibly resolved

The “best title” depends on company size. A 5-person installer and a 100-person regional group should not use the same role strategy.

## Evidence sources

- company website team/about/contact pages
- Companies House officers/PSC as identity evidence, not proof of current day-to-day responsibility
- professional/business profiles where permitted
- enrichment provider results

## Confidence

`HIGH`: role stated on current company site or reliable current business source.
`MEDIUM`: current enrichment + corroborating company evidence.
`LOW`: old/director-only evidence with no operational corroboration.

Do not personalize with irrelevant private details.
