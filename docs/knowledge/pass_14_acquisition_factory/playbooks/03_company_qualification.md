# 03 — Company qualification

## Initial W&D ICP hypothesis

Prefer firms that appear to:

- sell/install replacement windows and/or external doors to homeowners
- operate locally or regionally rather than being pure manufacturer/wholesaler
- have a functioning website and quote/contact flow
- have enough sales activity that a dormant quote database is plausible
- remain small enough that owner/director/commercial leadership is reachable
- have adequate operational capacity to fulfil recovered demand

## Signals

Positive:
- FENSA/Certass membership
- explicit installation services
- homeowner testimonials/reviews
- “get a quote” / survey CTA
- conservatories, bifolds, doors, windows as cross-sells
- local service-area pages
- multiple staff / showroom / sales function

Negative:
- manufacturer-only or trade-only
- repair-only/emergency-only
- new-build-only subcontractor
- dissolved/inactive company
- no evidence of homeowner sales
- obvious national enterprise where owner-level cold email is wrong motion

## Qualification output

Store `icp_pass`, `icp_reason_codes`, `icp_score`, and `qualification_version`.
