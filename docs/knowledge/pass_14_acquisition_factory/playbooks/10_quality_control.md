# 10 — Factory quality control

Monitor both throughput and correctness.

## Data-quality KPIs

- duplicate-company rate
- unresolved-entity-type rate
- no-domain rate
- contact-source provenance coverage
- email-valid rate
- accept-all/unknown share
- suppression-screen coverage
- stale-verification share
- manual-review backlog

## Acquisition KPIs

- sends
- delivery/bounce
- replies
- positive/research replies
- conversations
- exports requested
- exports received
- pilots proposed
- pilots accepted

## Downstream KPIs

Join to Pass 12:
- eligible homeowner records
- bookings
- attended surveys
- requotes
- sales
- cash collected

The acquisition factory is healthy only if downstream economics are healthy.
