# 7-day founder launch sprint

This is a learning sprint, not a promise that a client must close within seven days.

## Day 0 — readiness
- Clear the outbound-readiness gate.
- Load a first batch of prospects into `prospecting/prospect_input.csv`.
- Score and manually review the top prospects.
- Freeze the first outreach cohort and assigned variants.
- Confirm suppression/deletion workflow and reply ownership.

## Day 1 — first controlled sends
- Send a small, inspectable batch rather than the entire queue.
- Check actual delivery/bounce behavior before increasing volume.
- Record every reply class.
- Founder personally handles positive, ambiguous, legal/privacy and angry replies.

## Day 2 — conversation conversion
- Reply quickly to interested prospects.
- Move from curiosity to one concrete next step: short call or pilot-summary review.
- Research booked prospects with `research/pre_call_research.md`.

## Day 3 — calls + first follow-up
- Run calls with `sales/20_min_installer_call.md`.
- Ask for a redacted sample/export when fit exists.
- Send follow-up 1 only to prospects who have not replied and remain eligible.

## Day 4 — synthesize early evidence
- Review replies and calls.
- Diagnose the weakest funnel stage before changing copy.
- Record repeated objections and unexpected customer language verbatim.
- Do not pivot after three replies; update only strong evidence.

## Day 5 — second controlled batch
- Change one major variable only if evidence warrants it.
- Keep list quality, infrastructure and measurement stable enough to interpret results.
- Continue calls and export collection.

## Day 6 — follow-up + pilot conversion
- Send next scheduled follow-up where appropriate.
- For strong-fit prospects, use `sales/pilot_close.md`.
- Do not import or message homeowner data yet; route prospective Client 1 into Pass 12's gate.

## Day 7 — evidence review
- Run `tools/summarize_outreach.py`.
- Complete `learning/first_10_synthesis.md` once ten conversations exist; if not, continue the sprint until they do.
- Decide: keep offer, repair one stage, change segment, change channel, or pause.

## Founder time priority
1. Human replies / calls
2. Prospect quality
3. Evidence logging
4. Sending operations
5. Automation

Automation is last until the motion is understood.
