## Reply classes
### Positive / curious
Answer the direct question. Then ask one diagnostic next step:
- approximate old-quote volume;
- how it is stored;
- or a 15–20 minute call.

### “Send info”
Send the one-page pilot summary. Do not bury the prospect in a deck. End with one concrete question.

### “How much?”
Founder-pilot hypothesis from the current execution system: £0 setup and £75 per qualified booked survey, subject to the agreed definition and pilot gate. Do not present this as validated optimal pricing.

### “We already follow up”
Good. Ask what the actual process is: number/timing of touches, ownership, and whether every quote reaches a clear outcome. If systematic follow-up is genuinely strong, disqualify rather than force the sale.

### “GDPR?” / data question
Do not improvise a legal verdict. Explain that not every historical list is usable and that data provenance, suppressions, original collection route and contract/security controls are checked before any homeowner campaign.

### Explicit opt-out / negative
Suppress. Do not turn unsubscribe into an objection-handling exercise.

### Angry / legal / privacy / source question
Human only. Record exactly what was asked and how it was resolved.
