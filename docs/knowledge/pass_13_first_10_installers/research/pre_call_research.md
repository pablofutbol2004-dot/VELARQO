# Pre-call research — maximum 5 minutes

The call is for learning. Do not spend 45 minutes stalking a prospect.

Capture only evidence that changes the conversation:
- company + domain;
- service area;
- replacement products sold;
- quote/request-a-survey mechanism;
- likely decision-maker and role;
- company/entity information when confidently known;
- visible financing / showroom / trust scheme only if relevant;
- one reason the old-quote problem may plausibly exist.

## Do not do
- fake compliments;
- invent revenue/headcount;
- infer CRM from a website widget;
- assume they have a large database;
- assume follow-up is bad before asking;
- pitch based on scraped trivia.

## Opening preparation
Write one sentence:
`I want to understand how [company] handles [specific quote/follow-up process], especially what happens after a homeowner goes quiet.`
