# Prospect scoring

The score is a routing aid, not truth.

## Fatal conditions
Do not email when:
- `service_fit != yes`;
- `suppression_clear != yes`;
- `duplicate_clear != yes`;
- no usable email or no documented source.

## Positive signals
- homeowner-facing replacement installer;
- visible quote/survey flow;
- verified owner/director/sales leader;
- corporate entity confidently identified;
- service/product fit is explicit;
- multiple relevant product lines / meaningful local footprint.

## Uncertainty penalty
Unknowns are not treated as negative facts. They lower launch priority until verified.

## Priority bands
- A: strong fit, contact source documented, low ambiguity;
- B: fit likely, one or two important unknowns;
- C: weak/ambiguous; research before outreach;
- EXCLUDE: fatal condition.

The goal is to learn from clean cohorts, not maximize list size.
