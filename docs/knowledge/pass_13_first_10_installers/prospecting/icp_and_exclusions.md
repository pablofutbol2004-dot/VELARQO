# Initial installer ICP and exclusions

## Target hypothesis
UK homeowner-facing window/door installers that:
- sell replacement windows, external doors, bifolds, conservatories/solid roofs or close adjacencies;
- appear to generate and issue quotes/surveys rather than only subcontract installation;
- have enough local demand that old unclosed quotes plausibly accumulate;
- have a reachable owner/director/sales leader;
- have capacity to service recovered demand;
- are operationally able to export or otherwise inspect historical quotes.

These are hypotheses. Calls/exports determine whether they are true.

## Prefer initially
- independent/regional installers rather than national enterprises;
- clear homeowner-facing website;
- meaningful service area and multiple product lines;
- evidence of active lead generation / quoting;
- identifiable decision-maker;
- corporate business form where possible to simplify initial B2B email routing;
- business email that can be sourced and documented.

## Route separately / lower priority
- sole traders or ambiguous entity type: different PECR/privacy analysis may apply;
- pure manufacturers/wholesalers;
- commercial-only glazing contractors;
- emergency-only repair businesses;
- businesses with no apparent survey/quote sales process;
- firms outside target geography;
- firms already systematically following every quote to a clear outcome, unless another revenue-leak problem is visible.

## Hard exclusions for the current outreach experiment
- source of email/contact cannot be recorded;
- explicit previous opt-out/suppression;
- clearly irrelevant company;
- invented decision-maker or guessed personal data presented as fact;
- known closed/dormant business;
- duplicate entity already active in another sequence.

## Research fields that matter
Capture facts, not compliments:
- products sold;
- locations served;
- legal entity when known;
- decision-maker and role;
- lead/quote mechanism visible on site;
- trust/certification signals only when verified;
- any evidence of financing, quote request, showroom or survey flow.
