# 20-minute installer research + discovery call

## 0–2 — frame
“I mainly want to understand how enquiries and quotes move through the business, what happens when a quote goes quiet, and whether there is actually a sensible pilot here. If there isn’t, I’d rather know.”

## 2–6 — economics
Ask approximately:
- enquiries/month;
- surveys/month;
- quotes/month;
- jobs won/month;
- typical order value/range;
- current major lead sources;
- rough cost/effort of acquiring a new sale if known.

Do not pressure for exact financials. Record ranges and uncertainty.

## 6–10 — follow-up reality
- What happens after the quote goes out?
- When does the next touch happen?
- Then what?
- Who owns follow-up?
- When/why does the team stop?
- What percentage reaches a clear yes/no/not-now outcome?
- What reasons do homeowners give for delaying/not buying?

## 10–14 — backlog + systems
- Rough unclosed quote count?
- Oldest age worth considering?
- CRM / quoting software / spreadsheets / inbox / paper?
- Can you export name, contact, quote date, product, status, value?
- Are sold/lost/not-interested/opt-out statuses reliable?

## 14–16 — provenance + permissions evidence
- How are homeowner details normally collected?
- Website / phone / showroom / third-party leads?
- How are marketing preferences/opt-outs captured?
- Are bought/shared leads mixed in?

This is evidence collection, not a legal verdict.

## 16–18 — capacity + downstream process
- If five extra surveys appeared next week, could you handle them?
- How fast does someone confirm a booking/reply?
- Who attends?
- How will we get attendance, requote, sale and cash outcomes back?

## 18–20 — next step
If fit:
“Send me a redacted sample/export and the original capture/source information. I’ll audit what looks usable. If it passes the gate, we can run a bounded pilot rather than touch the whole database.”

If not fit, say why and end cleanly.
