# Pilot close protocol

Do not close before diagnosis.

## Fit conditions
The prospect should have evidence of:
- non-trivial dormant quote inventory;
- workable economics;
- export/data accessibility;
- understood data provenance enough to begin formal review;
- operational capacity;
- willingness to report downstream outcomes;
- willingness to honor the P0 gate.

## Founder pilot hypothesis
Current working commercial hypothesis:
- £0 setup;
- £75 per qualified booked survey;
- genuine no-show treatment defined in writing;
- bounded cohort, not the entire database;
- homeowner outreach only after Pass 12 gate clears.

Pricing remains an experiment until actual sales/economics validate it.

## Close language
“Based on what you’ve shown me, the next step isn’t a big contract or database blast. Send a redacted sample and the capture/source information. I’ll audit it against the pilot gate. If it passes, we agree the exact booking definition, message, cohort and stop conditions, then run small.”

## Never promise
- guaranteed sales;
- a legal conclusion before review;
- a specific response/booking rate;
- AI autonomy beyond approved rules;
- fake discounts or urgency;
- that every old contact is usable.
