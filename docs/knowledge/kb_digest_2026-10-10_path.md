## 1. Prior decisions and rankings about Velarqo

The later, blind-then-red-teamed `BUSINESS_TOP6.md` (which says it supersedes them) put it **fifth**: "biggest upside, most likely ~0", to be **time-boxed and started only after a first close in builder-for-hire or studio back-office**. `IDEAS_RESEARCH.md` scored it 24/50, seventh of nine. - **Red-team objections [D_redteam, TOP6].**
  - It reached #1 through prior attachment; the blind demand scan scored reactivation fit 4/10, missed calls 3, reviews 3, and called the density "competition, not demand".
  - It needs a stranger to hand over messy CRM data and to accept an outcome pitch from a 22-year-old abroad with no case study and no sales record. Coached sellers in the corpus show 0 of 15 calls in month 1, or 800,000 emails for 0 customers.
  - Jobber already bundles quote follow-up ($139/mo plan); a UK competitor (Honeybee) pitches AI reactivation to double glazing [IDEAS_RESEARCH].
  - UK GDPR and a data-processing agreement are needed for installers' customer lists.
  - Outcome shape (judgment, not data): 12-month net 0 to 1k euros (55%), 4 to 12k (35%), 20 to 40k (10%).
- **Conditions that would change the verdict.** A first close elsewhere; then the 14-day test: 150 personalised emails to UK Ltd installers, **pass = 5+ calls and 1+ deposit, kill = under 2 calls after one rewrite**, spend cap about 100 euros. Standing rule: no new research or digest files until 50 outreach touches are logged.
- **Money reality.** No option beats a 9 to 12 euros/hour job at month 3 or 6; the case for a business in year 1 is assets (sales skill, clients, niche knowledge) plus a right tail. **Week-8 checkpoint:** under 500 euros cumulative and no repeat client means take the part-time job and keep one 5 h/week test [TOP6].
- **LIFE_PLAYBOOK season goal and floors.** 8-week season (end date left blank); main goal first paid pilot. Floors: Velarqo = 15 min of follow-ups or 5 emails every weekday; uni = pass everything; body = 3 sessions/week and 7 h sleep; social = one real plan/week; content = YouTube only in a Sunday batch; admin = 20-minute weekly reset.

## 2. Expansion ladder evidence

Order repeated across files: dormant-quote recovery, then show-rate and confirmation, then fresh-lead speed and missed-call text-back, then **quote follow-up**, then reviews and referrals, then retainers, with ads last [speed_to_lead, home_services, meta_ads_local, jp_middleton].

- **Why the ladder, not the wedge, is the business.** Reactivation is front-loaded: the pool shrinks (8,000 records to about 500) and pay-per-recovery rewards leads dying; prevention (speed-to-lead, quote follow-up) is the better recurring product. Ratings: quote follow-up 9.5 potential, reactivation as wedge 8.5, standalone speed-to-lead 6, AI receptionist to start 5 [speed_to_lead, offers_pricing]. Windows are not emergencies, so quote follow-up and long nurture matter more than instant reply [home_services].
- **Retention evidence is thin.** The only worked retainer path is JP Middleton in gyms and franchises, self-reported. Clay Lawrence's postmortem is the counter-case: zero-risk pay-per-result brought the worst clients, unpredictable revenue and attribution fights [offers_pricing].
- **Price points (self-reported or CG unless stated).**
  - Reactivation: $499 first jobs, $1,500 on a live call, clients later at $1.5k to 2.5k/mo, some over $10k (JP Middleton; quotes verified, results not) [jp_middleton].
  - Per attended estimate: $150 plus $1k setup, or $2k for 15 appointments; $500 upfront plus $100 per appointment vs $2k/mo (Noah Haupt, home services) [offers_pricing].
  - Reviews and referrals: $250/mo per location on 12 months (JP) [jp_middleton].
  - CG: speed-to-lead 500 to 1,500 base plus per appointment; missed calls 300 to 1,000/mo; proven stage 1.5 to 3k/mo plus performance [chat_notes revenue_recovery, contractors].
  - Ceilings from software: Jobber quote follow-up $139/mo and AI receptionist $29/mo; UK Powered Now from 29, Sayora 49 [C_web_other, Zeus dossier]. Booked W&D appointments from about 130 (Lead Pronto, vendor); about 100 per qualified opportunity is "defensible" [chat_notes dbr_passes].
  - Value per appointment varies about 17x between installers, so price from each client's close rate and gross profit, not US numbers [offers_pricing].
- **Billing discipline.** Max two charging mechanisms; attended beats booked; no numeric guarantee until 5 to 10 campaigns; "30 sales in 30 days or 20k" reads as a scam to experienced owners [offers_pricing].
- **Timeline claims (CG).** 8 clients at 2.5k or 5 at 4k reaches 20k/mo and needs 100 to 200 emails/day; milestones are database access, a real recovered sale, a repeat, predictable clients [batch2_levers]. Architecture, not forecast.

## 3. Niche evidence

- **Why W&D/home improvement fits [home_services, offers_pricing].** Considered, quote-driven, competitor-shopped purchase; many "not now" quotes; customer still in the area; leads already paid for. Unsold quotes are the asset, not past buyers (low repeat). Verdict there: mechanism 8.5, UK W&D **about 7, provisional**; the corpus is US and does not validate W&D.
- **Good client:** spends on marketing, years of history, real unsold-quote pool, high job value, exportable data, capacity, a named sales owner. **Avoid:** sole traders (PECR consent), tiny databases, word-of-mouth-only, booked solid, bad reviews, no exportable data [home_services].
- **Size warning [IDEAS_RESEARCH].** Average W&D order about 3.7 to 3.9k (not 5 to 10k), average firm about 3.6 staff; only roughly 500k+/yr firms can afford 1.5 to 2.5k campaigns. Other files assume 3 to 30k jobs.
- **Later, conflicting evidence from Zeus (2026-10-09) [FINAL_VERDICT, home_services dossier].** UK windows/doors is "avoid as lead segment" (installations reportedly 4.3M in 2003 to 1.8M in 2024, vendor source, no dataset; weakest sub-trade on social). Narrow GO for **UK renewables installers** (heat pumps, solar, batteries): dated grant triggers and past-customer upsell, legally cleaner under soft opt-in. Garden rooms secondary. Owners say they need new customers, not follow-up; follow-up content gets tens of views.
- **Other niches.** Expansion list: kitchens, bathrooms, roofing, solar, heat pumps, insulation, stairlifts (CG Spain ranking: HVAC 9.5, windows 9.3). Clinics are the engine on niche #2, but UK botox cannot be advertised, data is special-category, market crowded [IDEAS, IDEAS_RESEARCH]. Zeus: car studios no-go (offer already sold, about 500 UK studios), clinics hold. **Avoid:** handyman (too low-ticket), plumbers/electricians (small, emergency, served by 29-pound receptionists), medical/legal first, anything US for a Spain-based sender.

## 5. Execution rules to reuse [LIFE_PLAYBOOK, watch_later_lessons]

- The strategist designs for the tired performer; daily check: "did I get real-world feedback today?"
- Sell block 60 to 90 min daily; Sell at least 50% of business hours until the first pilot; night-before 1 to 3 output tasks, each under 2 h with a definition of done.
- 12-word rules (e.g. "Before opening YouTube or VS Code, send today's five emails"); 20:00 racetrack with a stake; scary steps dosed at 4 to 7 out of 10.
- Pain trap: apathy or a better idea at the hard point is fog; one line in `parking.md`, read only on the decision date.
- Plan breaks about week 2: reset to actual average plus 20%, never a bigger plan. Pre-set storm days (exams) to floor; ramp back over 2 to 3 days.
- Monthly evidence review with numbers; kill criteria written before the data; pre-mortem and third-person framing at decisions. A pain point is a hypothesis until 3+ installers say it.
- Sunday review template; three-question check on any dead batch; recap email after every call; "waiting for" list.
- Start-now truths [batch3_start_now]: first goal is 1 euro from a stranger; cap the downside; no C8 until 6+ months of profit.
