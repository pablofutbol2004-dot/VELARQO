# Revenue-recovery offers and the €50k/month structure

**Source:** ChatGPT chat "DATABASE REACTIV" (6aa7c7c4), key answer saved 2026-10-05 before the chat was removed. These are ChatGPT's claims and haven't been verified; the vendor figures come from Lead Pronto and SholaX.

## Core idea
Build one **Revenue Recovery** company with several modules, not seven separate businesses. The pitch: "We find revenue you're currently losing and recover it."

1. **OLD MONEY:** database reactivation (still #1) and stale-quote recovery ("you have £482k of open quotes; we follow them until they buy, decline or are genuinely dead"). Charge per recovered sale or 5–10% of recovered revenue.
2. **CURRENT LEAKAGE:** speed-to-lead, missed-call recovery / AI receptionist, no-show and cancellation recovery, and follow-up.
   - Speed-to-lead: £500–1,500 base plus £X per qualified appointment or sale.
   - Missed calls: £300–1,000/mo plus £X per qualified appointment ("you already pay to make the phone ring").
3. **FUTURE MONEY:** past-customer expansion: cross-sell, upsell, maintenance, renewals, referrals. This is continuous rather than a one-off campaign.
4. **NEW MONEY (later):** pay-per-lead and pay-per-appointment demand generation. Riskier, because you pay to create the demand.

Every module reuses the same infrastructure: contacts, CRM, email/SMS/phone, workflows, AI qualification, calendar and attribution.

## Market evidence cited
- Lead Pronto (UK): booked home-improvement appointments from about £130, supplies about 100 companies, and offers revenue share on window/door leads.
- UK CRMs (SholaX) already highlight open-quote value and quotes with no follow-up, which shows the leak is recognized.

## The "free website" model
It's weaker on its own (you need many clients, churn is high, and a site isn't tied to money). Turn it into a **free conversion website + lead capture + instant response + booking**, charged monthly and/or per booked opportunity.

## Illustrative €50k/month (architecture, not a forecast)
- 5 reactivation campaigns × €4k = €20k
- 10 ongoing conversion clients × €1.5k base = €15k
- Performance fees across those clients = €15k

## The key line
Reactivate their old database first and make them money. Then: "Now give me every new lead automatically, so they don't become next year's reactivation database." That may be the whole business.
