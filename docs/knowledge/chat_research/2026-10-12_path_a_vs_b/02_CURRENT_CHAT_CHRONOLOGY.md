## 2026-10-11 19:32:48 UTC — User: quote recovery definition

> whats quote recovery¿

**Assistant answer (close transcript):** Quote recovery = help a company convert **estimates/presupuestos already sent but not accepted** into actual jobs. Example: roofing firm sends 50 estimates in a month; 15 accepted; 35 unclosed. Some prospects chose competitors, some found price too high, some forgot. Service identifies unsold estimates in CRM; permitted email/SMS/call follow-ups; helps answer objections or routes to salesperson; books appointments; tracks whether any incremental completed sales result.

Illustrative economics **35 unsold quotes**, **$12,000 average job**, **2 additional recovered jobs**, **$24,000 additional revenue**, **$750 monthly fee**. These were explicitly imaginary values, **not** roofing close-rate data; revenue ≠ gross profit. Software already offers basic auto-follow-ups; sell actual execution/performance, not the installation. Quote recovery is **subset** of larger lead conversion (missed calls, speed-to-lead, old leads).
