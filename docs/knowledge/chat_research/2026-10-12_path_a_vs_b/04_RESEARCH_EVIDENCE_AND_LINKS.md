**Critical:** This page archives what prior assistant replies **claimed** and cited; it is **not a fresh source verification performed for this export**. Some earlier cited results were internal ChatGPT search refs, which are not shareable URLs. Numbers may be vendor-sponsored, sample-dependent, and not broadly representative. Verify any quote/number before external publication or spending.

## A. Demand and market indicators

1. **Jobber Home Service Economic Report / 2026 survey:** reportedly sampled 1,050 US owners. Prior answer claimed **20%** responded to new leads within one hour; **40%** identified customer communication as time-consuming; **37%** identified quoting as time-consuming; roofing among sectors with stronger quote close rates. Earlier refs: `turn454671search0`, `turn241943search5`, `turn595513search3`. Caveat: wording/samples and which percentages represent 'most time-consuming' should be reconfirmed before publishing.
2. **ServiceTitan 2026 Roofing & Exterior report:** vendor survey >1,000 contractors; reportedly **75%** expect revenue growth, **16%** follow up unsold estimates same day, **41%** focus on marketing efficiency, **67%** respond to new leads within 4h. Earlier refs `turn595513search1`, `turn595513search2`. Caveat: vendor-sponsored survey; definitions and denominators need inspection.
3. **LocaliQ roofing/gutters search-ad cost per lead:** assistant cited **$228.15 median**, drawn from analysis of **3,211 home services campaigns** (the 3,211 was all analyzed campaigns, not necessarily roofing alone). Earlier ref `turn241943search0`. Caveat: paid-search sample, not actual company profitability or conversion.
4. **NAHB remodeling market:** earlier assistant cited positive US remodeling outlook for 2026 `turn450443search2`; macro outlook does not prove demand for quote recovery.

## B. Agency economics and outbound benchmarks

5. **Promethean Research 2026:** previously described agencies reducing offerings with average **30% net margins** versus **10%** for expanding offerings. Ref `turn454671search2`. Observational relationship ≠ causation. Also prior claim outbound email is one higher-rated channel for simpler agency cycles; referrals are important (`turn641666view0`).
6. **AgencyAnalytics 2026 agency survey:** previously cited **494 agencies**, with **55%** saying the most common client question related marketing performance to revenue. Ref `turn450443search4`; unclear survey sampling representativeness.
7. **Hunter State of Email Outreach 2026:** previously cited **31M emails** sent in 2025 and reply benchmarks **4.5% average sequence / ~3% sales**. Prior refs `turn968517search0`, `turn343565search6`. Read definitions: average reply ≠ positive reply ≠ booked calls ≠ revenue. Publisher website: https://hunter.io/blog/ (find original 2026 report there).
