## Why the recommendation narrowed

The business operator's strengths: programming/AI/automation, ability to build demos and systems, some budget but constrained attention and ~10–14 hours/week in prior context; does not want unbounded sales calls, physical walk-ins or content production before a business works. Eight warming inboxes are already an asset for B2B outbound. The client result needs to be both **valuable** and **credible** without existing case studies.

Broad revenue recovery was pitched as the category but the first sale needs an objective, tightly scoped process. Unsold quotes are an understandable start; everything else (new enquiries, calls, reviews, old databases, ads, full CRM) is **excluded from pilot scope** unless a buyer explicitly validates need and a clean delivery method.

## Proposed offer versions (chronology matters)

1. Earlier Path B lite: **$300–500 pilot + $500–800/mo** for enquiry handling/follow-up (inferred historical suggestion).
2. First current chat broad lead-conversion offer: **$750–1,500 setup + $500–1,000/mo**.
3. Later quote-recovery narrowing: **$400–800 30-day pilot**, and potentially **$500–1,000/mo** for real ongoing work; user has NOT accepted a final price and no actual buyer has validated these figures.

## Illustrative customer economics — NOT observed data

- 50 roofing quotes sent/month, 15 accepted, 35 unsold.
- Hypothetical 2 more jobs, $12k job value each → $24k **gross revenue**; a sample service fee $750/mo. This is not automatically net benefit: margins, attribution, taxes, project capacity and servicing costs matter.
- Other example: +2 jobs with $1.5k gross profit per job → $3k additional gross profit. This only makes sense if recovered sales really are caused by intervention.

## Warnings about software and delivery

- SaaS providers such as Jobber, AccuLynx and ServiceTitan may offer automated quote follow-up, text templates, scheduling and CRM functionality already. This isn't evidence all contractors have it configured or use it well.
- Merely setting up reminders and collecting a monthly fee is vulnerable to commoditization, churn and objections.
- Check technology installed, whether owners or staff actually use it, process leakage, and whether remedial service adds incremental revenue.
- Human sales staff must take over objections/complex quotes. No claim of fully autonomous revenue generation.
- Client data, consent and messaging laws matter; do not presume old quotes authorize arbitrary promotional SMS/email.
