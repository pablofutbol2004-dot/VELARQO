### Explicit user fears — prioritize these over agency optics

- **Can't close clients; no sales experience/case studies.**

### Material failure modes

- Insufficient monthly quote volume / tiny achievable lift.
- Existing staff CRM automation already handles follow-up well.
- Prospects already rejected quote for price, timing, alternatives; no incremental interest to recover.
- Contractor lacks fulfillment capacity or accurate job status data.
- Permission/consent/data access not appropriate for bulk contact.
- Buyer distrusts a new cross-border provider and won't hand over sales data.
- Clients don't track closed revenue correctly; performance pricing becomes disputed.
- Outreach messages land in spam or are not commercially relevant; high reply rate but no qualified buyers.
- Operating workload grows beyond solo capacity; monthly retention fragile after finite backlog cleared.

## 14-day discovery sprint suggested most recently

- **Days 1–3:** Audit competing agencies/software offerings and workflow detail; document where default CRM automation stops and additional operations begins.
- **Days 4–7:** Research and contact **50–100 genuinely qualified roofing businesses** using legally compliant outreach. Ask diagnostic questions: number of qualified quotes/month, lost quote reasons, current follow-up sequence, installed CRM, actual usage, price/margin of an incremental job, interest in outside help.
- **Days 8–14:** Do brief discovery/video calls, offer a narrowly scoped **paid 30-day pilot** with baseline metrics, handoff responsibilities and reporting. No need to build a broad product or content channel before buyer signal.

**Do not mistake zero sales from first 100 emails for conclusive market failure.** It is a weak small sample, heavily affected by list selection, email quality, timing and send/reply rate.

## Evidence thresholds / decision rules (new operational clarification, not earlier quoted agreement)

**Proceed** when multiple target owners independently confirm a material recurring process gap and at least one pays for a pilot with defined authorized delivery and credible economic upside.

**Adjust offer or ICP** when replies show owners don't have enough leads, the CRM already covers follow-up, problems concentrate on unqualified leads, or the owner cannot support extra jobs.

**Reconsider model** when repeated meaningful discovery says follow-up isn't a valued external service, buyers won't pay even after credible proof, or pilot operations are inefficient and unprofitable.

**Never infer** a success probability or likely monthly revenue from 8 inboxes or national industry averages alone.

## Avoid premature extensions

Do not automatically add paid ads, general content services, SEO, reputation, missed-call stack, custom app, or B2B outbound done-for-you scope to an initial quote-recovery test; each expansion demands separate buyer validation and delivery proof.
