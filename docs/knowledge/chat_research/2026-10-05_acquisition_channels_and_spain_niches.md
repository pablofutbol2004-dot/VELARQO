# Acquisition channel stack and Spain niche research

**Source:** ChatGPT chats "Rank UK Outreach Channels" (6aabdf98) and "Research Spain Niches" (6aa6d397), key answers saved 2026-10-05. These are ChatGPT's claims, cited to INE, OCU, UNEF, ASOFAP, ASEFAVE, BOE/RITE and ONTSI; I haven't verified them.

## Acquisition stack (Rank UK Outreach Channels)
1. **Cold email:** the core. It gives you volume and control, and you target the exact UK companies you want.
2. **LinkedIn / Instagram DMs:** secondary.
   - LinkedIn needs no followers, just a real, clear and credible profile.
   - On Instagram, 100–500 real followers and 6–12 decent posts or case studies are enough. A dead or empty account is the problem, not a small one.
3. **Partnerships and referrals:** potentially the strongest long term. Think CRM consultants, web/PPC agencies, lead-gen firms, installer software, industry consultants, suppliers/manufacturers, finance brokers and trade associations. One partner with 100 suitable clients beats months of cold outreach.
4. **Meta / paid ads:** only after proof (ICP, pain, offer, messaging and a tolerable CAC are known).

**Sequence:** cold email → add LinkedIn/IG touches → build partnerships hard → paid ads once an offer converts. Don't spend months building an audience; the profile only has to pass the "real, competent business" test.

## Spain niches (14 niches / 8 systems thesis), pass 2
- **Market:** about 389k active construction firms (INE, early 2025). About 81% have ≤2 employees and about 90% have ≤5. The market is huge, but most firms are too small.
- **ICP signals:** about 3–30 staff (or a subcontractor operation), €4k+ typical project, 10–20+ enquiries and 8–10+ quotes a month, 200+ historical leads, someone who answers the phone or WhatsApp, multiple lead sources, spare capacity, and an owner still selling. Headcount is only a proxy.
- **Quote conversion matters more than it looks:** OCU tells consumers to get at least 3 quotes, so issuing a quote means entering a competitive process. Track amount, competitor, finance need, objection, decision date, decision-makers, whether the proposal was viewed, revisions and stall reason, then pick the next action.
- **Financing is a big lever:** OCU says 71% don't know about subsidies. UNEF says 60–70% of residential solar is financed (about 80% for jobs over €10k or with batteries). Show cash price, monthly financing, aid and savings inside **quote conversion**, not as a separate system.
- **HVAC LTV:** RITE requires periodic maintenance, so the lifecycle runs install → maintenance → repair → replacement → add-ons → referrals. HVAC customer revenue moves up to 🔥🔥🔥.
- **Solar → aerothermia cross-sell:** 2 in 3 solar users plan to add aerothermia (UNEF 2025).
- **Pools:** €1.707bn in 2025 (+6.5%), some firms have 6 months of work booked, and labour is short. **The installed base beats lead gen.** Lifecycle triggers: old pump, manual chlorination, no cover or heating, older than 10 years.
- **Labour shortage changes the product.** More leads are useless at full capacity. Sell **opportunity prioritization**: value × close probability × margin × capacity fit ÷ effort.
- **Digital immaturity:** CRM use was 6.4% (22.7% at 10+ staff) in ONTSI's dated 2021 data. Reality is WhatsApp, Excel, PDFs and the owner's memory, so **commercial data ingestion** (normalizing the mess) could be part of the moat.
- **SATE/insulation** is a slow education-and-nurture sale (complexity, subsidy confusion, "€1 insulation" scams).
- **Associations as prospect lists:** ASEFAVE (165 members), AMC kitchens (160+), ASOFAP pools (250+).
