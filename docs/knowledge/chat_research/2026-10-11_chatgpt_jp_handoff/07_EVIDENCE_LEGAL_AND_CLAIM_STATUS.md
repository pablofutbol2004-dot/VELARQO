## Permission/compliance issues to research before an actual launch

- **UK PECR**: electronic marketing to individual subscribers typically needs valid consent or qualifying soft opt-in; the soft opt-in often requires offer of opt-out at collection and on every message. B2B limited-company rules differ; GDPR/UK GDPR still relevant if personal info processed. Verify current ICO guidance.
- **Spain/EU establishment**: being in Spain can trigger LSSI/GDPR requirements even when prospect is elsewhere. The user explicitly asked to ASSUME cold email is possible for planning, not to gloss over a potentially material legal issue.
- **US B2B email**: CAN-SPAM requirements include sender identity, postal address, opt-out and prompt compliance; cross-border issues and state laws merit checking.
- **US unsolicited SMS**: not a recommended initial acquisition channel because TCPA/FCC, state rules and A2P carrier policies can require consent and restrict platforms; don't present any blanket categorical rule for all individual manual texts.
- **Reviews/referrals**: do not encourage incentivized / selective / fake Google reviews; review-request flows must follow platform policy and FTC rules.
- **Health**: dental/med spa/PT workflows can involve HIPAA/PHI and marketing restrictions, add trust barriers for unproven operator.
- **Rental housing**: screening/qualification must comply with federal/state fair-housing rules.
- **Client new leads**: 'new' does not automatically mean consent for marketing. Transactional response to a request differs from unrelated promotional messaging. Configure each communication type and jurisdiction properly.
