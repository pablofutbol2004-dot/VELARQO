# Instruction to Claude: take over the decision, do not restart the idea spiral

You are taking over a conversation where a beginner founder wants to launch a *niche-focused B2B service agency* inspired by JP Middleton. User has 8 cold-email inboxes beginning ~Nov 1 2026, can allocate 20-30 total sends per inbox per working day and plans 2-touch sequences. They have no audience, experience or social proof. They are willing to take warm booked sales calls, but defer outbound live cold calling and do not want walk-ins. They are frustrated by recency bias and endless planning. They deliberately discarded earlier assumptions about UK windows/doors and old lead database reactivation.

## What the user needs from you

1. **Independently** identify 5-8 strongest niche×first-offer opportunities for a 0-proof beginner acquiring customers via cold email. You may include or exclude the 29 brainstormed niches; do not anchor to them.
2. Research *actual paying buyer* economics, pain frequency, availability of qualified companies/prospect data, competitor penetration and services, concrete operational outcome, ease of safely fulfilling with no credentials, and time-to-result. Verify current facts via credible primary sources where possible; cite URLs and dates. Make uncertainty explicit.
3. Explain **why one or two combinations outrank others** on *impact × feasiblity × distribution × trust friction*, not just TAM or high-ticket work. Choose a recommendation with a defensible first 30-day experiment rather than a false guaranteed winner.
4. Specify tangible deliverables and boundaries of the exact first offer, pilot and suggested fee structure, commercial value proposition, demonstration sample, who does which steps, tech complexity, data/compliance considerations, and what a client is *actually buying*.
5. Create a sample 2-touch cold email sequence grounded in real observed prospect facts (no fake case studies, false claims, exaggerated earnings), handling common objections, a 15-20-min booked call agenda, and minimal prospect qualification.
6. Quantify the maximum send/prospect capacity with correct follow-up accounting and create funnel scenarios as transparent assumptions. Establish measurable experiment gates: deliverability, replies, positive replies, booked calls, closes, result/pilot retention.
7. Respect legal scope: when assessing reactivation of *end-consumer* records, do not assume permission absent original opt-out/consent; cold B2B outreach is a different question. The user asks us to assume cross-border cold email is viable for commercial planning, but don't characterize that as legal clearance. Avoid recommending mass cold SMS.
8. Suggest where to get lists and how to verify 1,000+ right-sized target businesses cost-effectively; don't build elaborate infrastructure before validating the offer.
9. Keep the response direct and skeptical: user needs actionable conviction under uncertainty, not motivational rhetoric or speculative success probabilities.

## Key unresolved strategic tensions to address

- Reviews/referrals are JP's recommended beginner offer (low implementation difficulty), but commoditized software and legal limits may undermine pricing.
- Inbound enquiry conversion is measurable but requires customer already to have enough new leads and consented communications; trust/data access at signup.
- B2B appointment setting leverages founder's existing cold-email system but service delivery for clients would require extra legally compliant sending infrastructure and know-how.
- Paid ads can create demand but requires ad spend, real skill, and higher client risk.
- AEC/RFP workflow is demo-friendly but buyer ROI may be less directly measurable than booked appointments.
- Narrow vertical specialization helps sales proof and referrals but risks under-sized local segments or franchise vendor restrictions.
- Selling to the US gives potential budgets but competition/time-zone/cross-border administrative complexity.

## Research questions whose answers change the decision

- At what minimum inbound lead volume will appointment conversion justify $750/mo, net of new software and support?
- What is the plausible net profit of one incremental job/customer for a typical ICP? Do not equate contract revenue and profit.
- Are owners already paying for the functionality through Jobber/ServiceTitan/ArboStar/etc.? What is missing: tools, implementation, humans, monitoring, or no actual need?
- Can list filters reliably identify businesses with both budgets and gaps before cold emailing them?
- Can client staff actually follow up / deliver more appointments, or will the agency become a full-time dispatcher?
- How quickly can a beginner deliver demonstrable value without violating review, healthcare, or communication rules?
- Which niches have connected owner networks where happy clients can refer a specialist provider (a part of JP's actual growth)?

## Suggested answer shape

Start with recommendation and 1-2 paragraphs explaining uncertainty. Then a genuinely comparative niche-offer table (with evidence), one offer specification, a cheap 30-day test and failure diagnostics. Avoid asking the founder to research forever; choose with incomplete but sufficient evidence. The user is comfortable with direct disagreement.
