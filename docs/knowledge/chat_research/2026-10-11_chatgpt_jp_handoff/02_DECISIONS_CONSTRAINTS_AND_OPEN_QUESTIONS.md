## Unresolved decisions Claude SHOULD make explicit

1. Market (US / UK / another jurisdiction) with justified buyer economics, reachable prospects, competition, contact legality, time zones, trust requirements; US commercially attractive but not uniquely mandatory.
2. Specific ICP threshold (e.g., annual job volume, team size, site activity, inbound demand, client LTV, location, existing tooling).
3. First offer family: new inbound conversion, reviews/referrals, B2B appointment generation, customer retention, quote management, RFP support, etc.
4. Measurable outcome and customer payback; does it depend on existing lead volume, paid ads, appointments, staff close rate?
5. How to demo value honestly *without* client proof; pilot scope, measurement, price, risk reversal and fulfillment boundaries.
6. Outbound research and compliant sequence: list source, qualifying fields, sending schedule, unsubscribes and manual response handling.
7. Short kill/pivot criteria after a meaningful sample; distinguish deliverability, targeting, interest, booked calls, close rate, fulfillment, ROI.

## Bad assumptions to reject

- Biggest TAM => easiest first customer.
- High-ticket jobs => willing to buy an unknown agency's service.
- Low AI adoption => demand for AI automation.
- Automated text to old customer records is always okay because they requested a quote.
- A high email reply rate automatically means meetings or revenue.
- J.P.'s alleged revenue proves same results are typical.
- Three new niche ideas every day count as progress.
- If first 500 prospects fail, entrepreneurship itself is impossible.

## Suggested 30-day validation logic (NOT a user-approved binding plan)

- Pick one niche/buyer and a narrowly scoped offer based on comparative evidence.
- Build one honest demonstration, one landing page (optional), basic sales call process and 1,000+ verified prospect list.
- Start compliant, paced email sending; track delivered, replies, positive replies, meetings booked/showed, deals closed, retention and incremental client outcomes.
- Reassess after adequate sequence completion, not after a few days' vanity metrics.
- If no value/proof, do not scale volume merely because sending infrastructure exists.
