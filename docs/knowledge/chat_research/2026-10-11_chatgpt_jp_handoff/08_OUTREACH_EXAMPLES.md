If this isn't relevant, reply no and I won't follow up.

## Cautions

- These were proposed drafts for a suggested, not selected, niche. Do not treat tree care as decided.
- Personalization must be truthful: 'I noticed company offers estimates online' only if actually observed. Do not assert missed leads or quantifiable value without data.
- Add correct identification, required address/opt-out mechanism, privacy practices and suppression workflows under applicable law. A 'reply no' alone may not be adequate for every campaign setup.
- No fake testimonials, fake US address, fake results, or misleading 'RE:' follow-ups.
- A 2-touch sequence means initial + 1 follow-up, not 2 follow-ups.
