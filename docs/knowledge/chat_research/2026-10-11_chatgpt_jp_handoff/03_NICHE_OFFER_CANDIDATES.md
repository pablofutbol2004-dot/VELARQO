# Niche/offer landscape — brainstorming inventory, NOT a ranking

No market has been decided or objectively proven superior.

## Seven reusable offer archetypes

1. **Inbound lead -> booked appointment/estimate:** Route and respond to **newly permitted** leads, prevent dropped enquiries, reminders, reporting. Requires inbound volume. Good proof: speed-to-contact, appointments, job closes attributable to workflow.
2. **B2B qualified opportunities / appointment setting:** Find business buyers for a client's commercial service, outreach and handoff. Requires a buyer map, prospect data and sales experience. Avoid guaranteed appointments that are unqualified.
3. **Review requests + referrals:** JP recommends for beginners owing to simple delivery. Avoid incentivized/biased reviews; Google and FTC review policies apply. Harder to justify high retainers if it's merely SaaS setup.
4. **Quote conversion / pipeline:** Track currently active quotes and owner actions; communication permissions apply. Could be especially attractive when quotes have high values but long sales cycles.
5. **Retention & renewals:** Insurance, auto shop, subscriptions, maintenance. Strong lifetime value, harder immediate attribution.
6. **Tender/RFP discovery + draft preparation:** B2B/AEC firms; clear time-saving outcome, manual verification crucial, payback potentially uncertain.
7. **Recruitment admin:** Screening/appointment scheduling and follow-up with privacy-sensitive workflows; operational time saving, not necessarily direct incremental revenue.

## Suggested comparative matrix for actual selection

Research 5-8 shortlisted pairs; grade (0-5) with evidence: (a) buyer ability/willingness to pay, (b) painful frequent gap NOT already solved, (c) economic uplift per unit outcome, (d) ease of initial fulfillment without credentials, (e) presence and reachability of real decision-makers, (f) cold-email message differentiation, (g) sales cycle, (h) legal/technical risks, (i) availability of 1,000 qualified leads, (j) ability to quantify pilot result inside 30-45 days.

Do not collapse different services into 'AI agency.' Sell the **specific business outcome** and be explicit about ongoing management versus one-time software installation.
