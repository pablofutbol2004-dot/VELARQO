# Prospecting mechanics: 8 inboxes, 2-touch cold email, optional future calls

## Exact daily and monthly email math

Let `I = inboxes`, `S = total sends per inbox per day (includes ALL follow-up emails)`, `D = sending days`, and `T = sequence emails per prospect`.

`total_email_slots = I × S × D`

`theoretical_completed_prospects = total_email_slots / T` (steady-state / fixed-ratio approximation, not necessarily cohorts fully completed that same calendar month).

| Sending config | 20-day month | November 2026, 21 weekdays |
|---|---:|---:|
| 8 inboxes × 20 sends/day × 2 touches | 1,600 unique prospects | 1,680 |
| 8 inboxes × 25 sends/day × 2 touches | 2,000 | 2,100 |
| 8 inboxes × 30 sends/day × 2 touches | 2,400 | 2,520 |
| 8 inboxes × 30 sends/day × 3 touches | 1,600 | 1,680 |

**Warnings:** Warm-up, domain health, invalid addresses, bounce rate, send limits, holidays, response-driven suppression, staggered steps, not working weekend, and sequences spilling into next month change realized results. Sending 30 per inbox is a target/assumption, not permission or deliverability guarantee. If the first follow-up sends 4 days later, first-month NEW outreaches and that month's completed sequences are distinct metrics.

## Acquisition channel choices

- **Phase 1 (agreed):** cold email to carefully chosen B2B decision-makers (assuming legally permitted), manual response handling, booked Zoom/Meet sales calls. No cold SMS and **no outbound cold calls** at launch.
- **Possible phase 2:** 10-20 targeted live cold calls/day to well-qualified businesses *already in email list*, if user opts in to that channel; not automatically.
- **Other avenues:** niche partnerships and software implementers, manual LinkedIn outreach where owners are active, marketplaces with buyer intent, niche-specific webinars/content later, permissioned referrals and postal direct mail to high-value contacts.

## Distinguish types of calls

- *Cold call:* user calls a stranger to initiate commercial conversation; deferred at start.
- *Sales call:* buyer who responded positively to email agrees to a consultation; user WILL do these.
- *User stated NO walk-ins:* avoid suggesting uninvited in-person visits.

## Funnel (all hypothetical, do not present as an empirical prediction)

Prior assistant used 1,000 contacts -> 3% all replies (30) -> 20% positive of replies (6) -> ~65% book (4) -> 25% close (1). These percentages were not validated for the niche and could produce zero customers. Use this **only** to show the mathematical relationship among stages. Segment total replies vs positive replies and actual revenue.
