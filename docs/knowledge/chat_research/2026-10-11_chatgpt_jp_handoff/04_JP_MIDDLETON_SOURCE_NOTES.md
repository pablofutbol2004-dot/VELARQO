## His advice for someone starting fresh

JP says he would start with **AI reviews and referrals** because it's relatively straightforward to fulfill and perceived to deliver visible results. In `How I would start an AI Agency in 2025 (If I could Start Again) [V-aN_i0GK9Q].txt`, he mentions **$250–500 per month** as his prices for some such customers. Don't generalize into a verified price benchmark or guaranteed client ROI.

Caveat: transcript descriptions include incentive/raffle-based review solicitation; such tactics may conflict with Google's restrictions on incentivized reviews and could raise FTC concerns. Do not copy them unquestioningly.

## Critical transfer lesson

His competitive advantage was not a secret AI stack. It was niche specialization, strong offers and sales, operational results, standardization, and especially distribution through trusted industry relationships/franchise networks. Those relationships are neither instantaneous nor guaranteed. A new founder could adopt the *sequence* without assuming the same success or legality of every tactic.
