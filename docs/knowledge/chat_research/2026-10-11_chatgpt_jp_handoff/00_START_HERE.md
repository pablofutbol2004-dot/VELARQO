# Claude handoff — JP Middleton-style agency from zero

Date: 2026-10-11. Source: current ChatGPT conversation plus user's original JP Middleton transcript archive. Start here. Treat this as a decision/research handoff, NOT as a validated business plan.

## Most important state

**Clear and agreed:** User wants to attempt a niche-focused B2B services/agency model drawing on JP Middleton's principles. The user has 8 cold-email inboxes expected to become usable from **November 1, 2026**, plans **20–30 total outgoing emails per inbox per sending day**, and **2 email touches per company** (initial + follow-up; 3 touches has been considered). No audience, clients, testimonials, proven skills, or validated offer; user's explicitly stated starting point is zero. User will conduct inbound/qualified sales calls over video; **does not want outbound cold calling in the initial phase**, though might reconsider later. No in-person walk-ins. Cold email is the intended primary prospecting method. User wants results and will learn skills necessary to obtain them.

**Not decided:** Country, niche, buyer profile, offer, price, delivery method, initial guarantee, tech stack, conversion rates, anticipated earnings. Prior assistant suggested US tree care, gyms, HVAC, etc., but those are **ideas, not decisions**. User explicitly rejects anchoring to UK windows/doors, old reactivation projects, or whichever industry was mentioned last. Avoid recency bias.

**Important legal/permission distinction:** User reports investigating UK old-quote reactivation and finding that many firms lack the original opt-out required for a UK PECR soft opt-in; reports '96%' from prior work, not independently validated here. Therefore do **not assume reactivation of consumers' old quotes via email or SMS is lawful**. This is *different* from cold B2B outreach to business owners. For planning, user requested 'let's assume I can send cold email from Spain'; this is a modeling premise, not legal clearance. US cold SMS is not a recommended initial channel (complex TCPA/state rules and messaging-provider opt-in policies).

## Confirmed outreach math

- 8 inboxes × 30 TOTAL sends/inbox/weekday = **240 emails/weekday**, including sequence follow-ups.
- Assuming 20 sending days in a representative month: 4,800 emails / 2 emails per company = **2,400 unique prospects/month**, at steady state, assuming all touches allocated within the volume.
- At 20 sends/inbox/day and 20 days: 3,200 / 2 = **1,600 unique prospects/month**.
- With 3 touches at 30 sends: 4,800 / 3 = **1,600 unique prospects/month**.
- November 2026 has 21 Mon–Fri dates; at max 30/inbox/day = 5,040 send slots -> **2,520** theoretical 2-touch prospects (steady state), but follow-up timing, public holidays, warm-up, deliverability and spillover into December lower realized cohort completion. November 1, 2026 is Sunday.
- If a user says '30 emails a day' clarify only if necessary: in this discussion it means **30 TOTAL emails PER inbox PER day**, NOT 30 new prospects, and follow-ups count.

## Business logic

- Copy *principles*, not JP's outcomes: niche, an expensive measurable bottleneck, simple first offer, direct prospecting and sales, good fulfillment, testimonials, standardized expansion, referrals/industry networks.
- Sales outcome with zero proof is uncertain; do not project income as certain or treat cold email reply rates as booked-meeting rates. Begin with one defensible market-offer hypothesis and a bounded experiment.
- Treat proposed retainer prices as guesses to validate, not actual market-clearing prices.
- A sufficiently useful next response from Claude should **recommend and justify a small set of niche-offer combinations using evidence, funnel mechanics, ease of fulfillment, channel fit and ROI**, and then a single concrete test; do not regress to unlimited idea brainstorming.
