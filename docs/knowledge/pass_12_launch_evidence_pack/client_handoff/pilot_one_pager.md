# Old-quote recovery pilot — working one-pager

## Goal
Re-contact an approved segment of older homeowner quotes, identify people still interested, and route qualified opportunities back into your sales calendar/process.

## Pilot scope
- start with a small approved segment rather than the whole database;
- agree exactly which products, geography and quote ages are in scope;
- exclude existing customers/active opportunities and honour suppression/opt-outs;
- agree copy/offers before launch;
- track outcomes through booked survey, attendance, requote and sale where possible.

## What we need from you
1. A small export/sample of relevant quote records.
2. Confirmation of how the records were collected and how opt-outs/objections are stored.
3. A named person responsible for replies/bookings.
4. Enough appointment capacity during the pilot.
5. Confirmation of downstream outcomes so results can be measured accurately.

## What happens before any campaign
We audit the data structure and eligibility, agree the billable result and exclusions, test routing/suppression, and run a small canary before expanding.

## Measurement
A “reply” is not automatically a result. We track the downstream funnel and reconcile outcomes with you.

This is a pilot specification, not a legal opinion or guarantee of results.
