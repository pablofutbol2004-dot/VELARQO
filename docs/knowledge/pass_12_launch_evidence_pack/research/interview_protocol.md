# Installer interview protocol

The goal is not validation. The goal is to discover where the thesis is wrong.

## Workflow first
“Talk me through the last homeowner enquiry from first contact to either sale or lost quote.”

Probe:
- source of enquiry;
- who calls first;
- survey booking;
- survey/visit;
- how quote is produced;
- how quote is sent;
- exact follow-up sequence;
- when it becomes “lost”;
- whether anyone revisits it later.

## Volumes
Ask for actual recent-month numbers where available, not “roughly busy”.
- enquiries;
- surveys;
- quotes;
- wins;
- average job value;
- current capacity.

## Dormant inventory
- where old quotes live;
- how many;
- age distribution;
- fields present;
- how statuses are represented;
- whether source/marketing provenance exists;
- whether opt-outs/objections are stored.

## Economics
- what they pay for leads / Google / lead sites / canvassing / showrooms;
- value of an attended survey;
- sales close rate from attended survey or requote;
- margin/contribution language they actually use.

## Failure modes
- why quotes die;
- what happens when lead volume spikes;
- missed calls / slow response;
- salesperson/office bottlenecks;
- prior agency burns.

## Buying
- what would make this worth testing now;
- what would make them refuse even if results sound good;
- who else needs to approve it.

## End with evidence request
If appropriate, ask for a small **redacted export sample** showing field structure and statuses before requesting full campaign data.

## Interview discipline
Do not explain the product before understanding the workflow unless necessary. Capture concrete examples and exact language. Separate observed facts from the interviewee's estimate/opinion.
