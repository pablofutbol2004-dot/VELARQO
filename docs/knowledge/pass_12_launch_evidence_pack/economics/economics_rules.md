# Economics rules

## Velarqo
Track actual cash and direct cost, not just booked revenue.

`contribution = cash/earned fees − direct channel/API cost − other direct cost − costed founder labour (for capacity analysis)`

Track payment lag and disputed/credited results separately.

## Client
At minimum calculate:
- fee / qualified booked survey;
- fee / attended survey;
- fee / won job;
- attributed and, where possible, incremental revenue / fee.

Revenue is not profit. If the installer will share contribution/gross-margin information, use it to estimate value more honestly.

## Pricing learning
Do not change price because a single prospect objects. Record objections and compare close rate, result quality, disputes and margin across enough evidence to distinguish price from weak value/proof.
