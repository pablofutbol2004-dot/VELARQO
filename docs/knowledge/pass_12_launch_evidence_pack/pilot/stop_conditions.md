# Pilot stop conditions

These are guardrails, not universal market benchmarks.

Stop immediately or pause the next batch if any of the following occurs:
- suppression/opt-out mechanism fails;
- records appear to come from an unapproved or materially different source than represented;
- client cannot support a promised offer/message;
- replies reveal systematic wrong-person/incorrect targeting;
- technical routing loses replies or bookings;
- client operational failure makes bookings unusable (e.g. no availability, repeated missed appointments);
- a material compliance concern emerges that the Pass 02 gate does not resolve;
- negative sentiment or complaints are materially outside the pre-written tolerance;
- manual handling becomes so high that continuing would teach little without process redesign.
