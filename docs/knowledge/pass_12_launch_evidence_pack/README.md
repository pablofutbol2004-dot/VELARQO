# Pass 12 — Launch Evidence Pack / Client-1 Gate

This pass is the transition from researched business knowledge to **first-party evidence**.

The operating rule is simple:

> no assumption gets promoted because it sounds plausible; it gets promoted because a specific interview, export, send, booking, attended survey, quote, sale, payment, or controlled comparison supports it.

## What this pack does

It creates one instrumented path:

`prospect → installer conversation → export → P0 gate → eligibility audit → canary → micro-pilot → attendance → requote → sale → cash → post-mortem → claim update`

It deliberately does **not** try to automate the whole business before Client 1.

## Minimum evidence targets before strong conclusions

- 10 substantive installer conversations;
- 3 real/redacted quote exports from different firms if possible;
- 1 client passing every non-waivable P0 gate;
- 1 controlled micro-pilot with contact-level event capture;
- downstream reconciliation through attendance, requote, sale and payment;
- holdout/baseline measurement when feasible;
- manual-time and exception tracking.

## Key folders

- `launch/` — exact stage sequence and launch board.
- `prospecting/` — prospect evidence schema, not a vanity CRM.
- `research/` — interview capture and synthesis.
- `client_gate/` — machine-checkable P0 gate.
- `data/` — export audit, provenance and eligibility.
- `pilot/` — config, event ledger, canary and downstream reconciliation.
- `measurement/` — causal integrity, cohorts, definitions and baselines.
- `economics/` — Velarqo/client economics and billing evidence.
- `learning/` — empirical question map, evidence ledger and decision log.
- `tools/` — scripts to create a Client-1 workspace, validate readiness and analyze a pilot.
- `client_handoff/` — clean client-facing request/checklist documents.

## Non-waivable principle

A weak data-provenance, suppression, security, capacity, measurement or safety gate cannot be overridden because the commercial opportunity looks attractive.
