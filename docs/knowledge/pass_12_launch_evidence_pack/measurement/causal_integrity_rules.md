# Causal integrity rules

- Define the primary outcome before launch.
- Keep denominators stable.
- Preserve a frozen eligible cohort.
- Record all exclusions before looking at outcomes where possible.
- Keep holdout assignment immutable.
- Record cross-channel contamination rather than silently dropping it.
- Do not mark an outcome “campaign caused” merely because it happened later.
- Track cancellations/refunds/non-payment after the apparent win.
- Report sample sizes next to every rate.
- Segment only on variables known before treatment unless clearly labeled exploratory.
- Do not choose the best-performing quote-age bucket after the fact and present it as the original strategy.
