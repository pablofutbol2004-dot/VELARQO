# Critique and QA

Every generated design should undergo a critique pass.

## Score 1–10

- Hierarchy
- Readability
- Composition
- Alignment
- Spacing
- Typography
- Contrast
- Color
- Imagery
- Consistency
- Originality
- Restraint
- Narrative
- Format fit

## Critique Prompt

Identify the three highest-impact visual flaws.

Fix those first.

Do not redesign elements that already work.

## QA Questions

- Is the primary message immediately obvious?
- Can the design be scanned quickly?
- Is anything decorative but unnecessary?
- Are alignments intentional?
- Is spacing semantically meaningful?
- Are line breaks strong?
- Is the palette controlled?
- Does imagery reinforce the message?
- Does the design feel like one system?
- Does it fit the medium?
