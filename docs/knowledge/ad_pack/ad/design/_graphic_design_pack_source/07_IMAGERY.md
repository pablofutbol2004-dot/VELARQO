# Imagery

Images are compositional ingredients, not objects to merely fit into boxes.

## Evaluate

- subject placement
- eye direction
- negative space
- contrast
- cropping
- dominant colors
- detail density
- visual energy

## Cropping

- Crop to strengthen composition.
- Avoid awkward body or limb cuts.
- Preserve important faces.
- Consider eye-line and gaze direction.
- Use aggressive crops when they improve scale and impact.

## Image + Text

Prefer quieter image regions for text.

When text must sit over noisy imagery, use:
- local darkening
- gradient
- scrim
- blur
- contrast adjustment

## Rules

- Imagery should support the message, not merely decorate it.
- Prefer strong, specific photography over generic stock-style imagery.
- Documentary-feeling imagery can increase credibility in case studies.
