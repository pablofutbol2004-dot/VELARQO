# Design Philosophy

## Core Principle

Graphic design is controlled attention.

The job of a design system is to control:
1. What the viewer notices first.
2. What they notice second.
3. How easily they understand the message.
4. What they feel.
5. What they do next.

## Priority Order

When improving a design, work in this order:

1. Message clarity
2. Visual hierarchy
3. Composition
4. Typography
5. Spacing and alignment
6. Imagery
7. Color
8. Decoration
9. Micro-polish

Never spend time polishing decoration while the core message or hierarchy is weak.

## General Rules

- One dominant idea per frame or section.
- Large visual differences are usually stronger than many subtle differences.
- Every element should justify its existence.
- Empty space is an active design tool.
- Consistency creates a system; deliberate rule-breaking creates emphasis.
- Design should optimize communication before ornament.
- Avoid “medium everything.”
- Avoid generic AI aesthetics unless conceptually justified.
