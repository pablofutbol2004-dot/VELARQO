# Information Design

Good graphic design compresses meaning visually.

## Transform Text Into Visual Form

Consider replacing paragraphs with:

- charts
- comparisons
- timelines
- diagrams
- matrices
- funnels
- before / after
- annotated screenshots
- metric cards
- process maps

## Example

Instead of:
“74% of workshops fail to respond to leads within five minutes.”

Try:
74%
DON'T RESPOND
WITHIN 5 MINUTES

Then use smaller supporting explanation.

## Rule

Always ask:
Can this be communicated visually instead of as another paragraph?

## Data Discipline

- Emphasize the key number.
- Keep labels clear.
- Avoid decorative charts with no analytical value.
- Use visual encoding consistently.
