# Style Systems

Do not describe styles only with adjectives such as “modern,” “premium,” or “bold.”

Translate style into operational rules.

## Style System Template

### Typography
- families
- weights
- headline treatment
- tracking
- leading

### Composition
- grid
- symmetry / asymmetry
- negative space
- cropping

### Color
- palette
- accent behavior
- saturation rules

### Imagery
- subject matter
- photographic treatment
- contrast
- grain
- crop behavior

### Decoration
- rules
- icons
- borders
- radii
- shadows
- motifs

### Rhythm
- dense vs sparse sections
- repetition
- pattern interruption

## Example

Typography:
- grotesk display
- extreme scale contrast
- tight headline leading

Composition:
- asymmetric editorial grid
- large negative space
- occasional edge cropping

Color:
- monochrome foundation
- one saturated accent

Imagery:
- documentary automotive photography
- slightly desaturated
- high contrast

Decoration:
- thin rules
- micro labels
- no generic gradients
- no decorative 3D blobs
