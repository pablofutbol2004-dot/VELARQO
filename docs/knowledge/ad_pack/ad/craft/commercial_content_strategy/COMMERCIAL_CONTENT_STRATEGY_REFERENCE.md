# Commercial Content Strategy Craft — Reference

## 1. Purpose
Owns the portfolio-level craft of making organic content commercially useful: assigning jobs to content streams, balancing reach with qualified attention, sequencing complementary roles, preserving message-to-offer continuity, and feeding market learning back into the content system.

## 2. Core model
Commercial content is a portfolio. Different assets can create discovery, diagnose a problem, educate, build trust, prove capability, resolve doubts, qualify, or invite action. The strategy layer decides which jobs the system needs and how those jobs fit together; it does not re-own the mechanics of proof, CTAs, funnels, offers, or measurement.

## 4. Canonical rules

### Commercial Role And Portfolio

- **Rule:** Give each content pillar or recurring series an explicit commercial or relationship job instead of treating publishing volume as the strategy. **Why:** A portfolio becomes steerable when each stream exists for a reason such as reach, education, trust, lead capture, conversion, or retention rather than because the format is available. **Source:** [C12K:eXFp3cWkce4@00:09:58] [C12K:eXFp3cWkce4@00:10:52]
- **Rule:** Let different content streams perform different jobs; do not force every piece to maximize reach, teach deeply, build trust, and sell at the same time. **Why:** Reach, affinity, expertise, lead capture, and direct action can coexist in one system, but combining every objective in every asset creates conflicting design pressures. **Source:** [C12K:eXFp3cWkce4@00:10:23] [C12K:eXFp3cWkce4@00:11:21]
- **Rule:** Allocate content volume according to the current business bottleneck or strategic objective rather than maintaining equal output across every content job. **Why:** A portfolio optimized for audience expansion should not have the same mix as one optimized for conversion, trust, or retention. **Source:** [C12K:eXFp3cWkce4@00:11:41]
- **Rule:** Define what the content system is trying to accomplish before choosing recurring topics, series, or production volume. **Why:** Without an intended outcome, creators can produce consistently while wandering between incompatible goals. **Source:** [C12K:Ch4Sl0POBhU@00:05:31] [C12K:x8kSE70o_qE@00:04:57]

### Reach And Qualified Attention

- **Rule:** Treat broad-reach content as an entry point, not proof that the commercial content system is working. **Why:** A broad piece can inject new people into the audience while narrower content performs the later work of filtering, educating, building authority, and converting. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:12:40] [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:14:01]
- **Rule:** Prefer qualified attention over raw attention when the objective is commercial, even if the qualified audience is smaller. **Why:** Views from people who are unlikely to need, influence, or buy the offer can inflate reach without improving the business outcome. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:44:17] [C12K:F0-yC1gVElA@00:54:32]
- **Rule:** Use broader topics only when they still overlap the intended buyer and there is a credible downstream path that narrows toward the commercial problem. **Why:** Broadness can expand discovery, but unrelated virality fills the system with attention that later content cannot convert coherently. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:12:40] [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:15:20]
- **Rule:** Meet prospects with a problem or desired improvement that is salient now, then bridge that immediate concern toward the larger problem the offer solves. **Why:** A stepping-stone problem can earn attention from the right market before the buyer is ready to engage with the full commercial outcome. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:17:56]

### Sequence And Progression

- **Rule:** Design the content portfolio so successive pieces can perform complementary commercial jobs rather than expecting one asset to complete the entire sale. **Why:** Commercial decisions often require multiple exposures in which awareness, belief, authority, proof, objection resolution, and action can accumulate. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:14:01] [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:35:59]
- **Rule:** After a broad attention event, deliberately publish the deeper content needed to convert that new attention into understanding, trust, and intent. **Why:** A reach spike creates an audience opportunity, not a completed sales process; follow-on content determines whether the new attention becomes commercially useful. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:35:59] [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:36:28]
- **Rule:** Use content before the offer to reduce uncertainty and prepare decisions instead of reserving all persuasion for the final pitch. **Why:** Teaching, proof, belief work, and objection resolution can pre-sell the logic of the decision so the commercial ask does not carry the full burden alone. **Source:** [C12K:pr5ZPbCDyK0@00:08:28] [C12K:pr5ZPbCDyK0@00:24:41]

### Message And Offer Continuity

- **Rule:** Keep social content, deeper owned content, and the paid offer conceptually congruent so each step feels like a deeper continuation of the same problem and solution space. **Why:** A prospect progresses more easily when the next asset expands the same promise and reasoning instead of abruptly switching topics. **Source:** [C12K:sfABlt-pTd0@00:35:55] [C12K:sfABlt-pTd0@00:36:58]
- **Rule:** Make the public content reveal the kind of thinking, mechanism, or capability the offer actually delivers rather than building an audience around unrelated entertainment. **Why:** Content that previews the real commercial value creates a more informed and better-matched audience for the offer. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:36:28] [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:40:12]
- **Rule:** Do not manufacture a content promise that the offer or delivery cannot continue. **Why:** Commercial continuity breaks when the content creates expectations that the product, service, or customer experience cannot satisfy. **Source:** [C12K:sfABlt-pTd0@00:35:55] [C12K:sfABlt-pTd0@00:36:58]

### Market Feedback Loop

- **Rule:** Feed recurring audience questions, objections, sales conversations, and customer results back into the commercial content roadmap. **Why:** The market continuously reveals the misunderstandings, doubts, language, and proof that future content should address. **Source:** [C12K:_5RQAjOyG10@00:11:40] [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:40:12]
- **Rule:** Rebalance the content portfolio when downstream audience quality or commercial response shows that a high-performing content stream is attracting the wrong people. **Why:** A content stream can win on reach while weakening the business if its audience does not progress into relevant interest or fit. **Source:** [C12K:cXBJSYL5_2E@00:44:17] [C12K:F0-yC1gVElA@00:54:32]

### Decision Oriented Education

- **Rule:** Use educational content to improve a buyer decision, not merely to maximize the amount of information delivered. **Why:** A larger information dump can increase cognitive load without changing what the prospect understands, believes, or does. **Source:** [C12K:pr5ZPbCDyK0@00:24:41] [C12K:pr5ZPbCDyK0@00:28:10]
- **Rule:** When content has a commercial job, choose teaching points that clarify the problem, mechanism, tradeoff, proof, or decision relevant to that job. **Why:** Interesting but commercially unrelated teaching can provide value while leaving the purchase logic unchanged. **Source:** [C12K:pr5ZPbCDyK0@00:25:43]

### Demand Creation And Capture

- **Rule:** Distinguish content that captures already-active demand from content that must create problem or solution awareness before a buyer is ready to evaluate an offer. **Why:** Someone already seeking a solution can receive direct comparison and action content, while latent demand requires diagnosis, education, or opportunity framing first. **Source:** [C12K:Qmu8t2fh_zM@00:02:00] [C12K:Qmu8t2fh_zM@00:03:15] [C12K:ZqQaILCfYVI@00:32:36]
- **Rule:** Do not assume content can manufacture demand for every offer; when purchase depends on an external event or pre-existing operational condition, prioritize finding the people who already exhibit that need. **Why:** Some purchases are contingent on a trigger or prerequisite, so broad persuasion of people without the condition wastes attention compared with demand capture or tighter targeting. **Source:** [C12K:Qmu8t2fh_zM@00:03:38] [C12K:e4jTkcdDwVI@00:17:09]
## 5. Important tensions and resolutions

- Broad reach vs qualified attention: broad discovery is useful only when it overlaps the target market and can feed a narrower commercial path.
- Single-piece persuasion vs ecosystem persuasion: a piece may have one job; the portfolio carries the full commercial burden.
- Value vs selling: commercially useful teaching should improve buyer decisions, but not every useful piece needs a direct pitch.

## 6. Corpus claims deliberately not promoted into universal rules

- No fixed TOFU/MOFU/BOFU percentage or posting ratio is universal.
- No universal number of touches or days before conversion was accepted.
- Virality is not treated as inherently good or bad; its value depends on audience quality and downstream behavior.

- `cta_conversion` owns the requested next action.
- `funnel_mechanics` owns journey states, paths, handoffs, nurture, and bottlenecks.
- `content_measurement` owns metrics, experiments, and diagnostic measurement.
- `offer_framing` owns the commercial object itself.
