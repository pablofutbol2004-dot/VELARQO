# Expansion Research Reference

## Method
- Primary business-domain corpus: 11,917 C12K transcripts.
- Candidate routing used domain-specific phrase searches, then high-signal transcript anchors were used as provenance for canonical rules.
- AI-systems research supplemented the transcript corpus with current primary engineering sources from Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS, and NIST.
- The goal was not to preserve every creator tactic; it was to extract durable operating principles and reject unsupported absolutes.

## AI primary sources
- Anthropic — Building effective agents
- OpenAI — A practical guide to building AI agents
- OpenAI — Evals API/docs
- AWS — Retry with backoff pattern
- AWS Builders' Library — Making retries safe with idempotent APIs
- NIST — AI Risk Management Framework

## Important synthesis choices
- paid_media excludes creative craft owned by ad_creative.
- brand_strategy excludes positioning and generic visual craft.
- reputation_reviews excludes underlying proof principles.
- unit_economics excludes price-setting mechanics owned by pricing.
- crm_lifecycle excludes qualification criteria and funnel-state economics.
- ai_systems is intentionally vendor-neutral in its rules even when provenance includes vendor documentation.
