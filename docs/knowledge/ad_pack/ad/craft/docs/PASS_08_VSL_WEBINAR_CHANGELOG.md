## Classification decisions
- VSL and webinar remain separate format packs despite overlapping sales structures.
- VSL is defined by asynchronous sequential consumption.
- Webinar is defined by event/session states and the possibility of live interaction, attendance, Q&A, replay, and event-specific follow-up.
- Fixed runtimes, fixed pitch timestamps, benchmark conversion rates, and tool-specific tactics remain non-canonical.
