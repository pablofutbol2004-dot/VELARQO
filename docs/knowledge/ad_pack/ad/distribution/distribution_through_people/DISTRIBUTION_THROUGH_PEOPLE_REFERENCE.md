# DISTRIBUTION_THROUGH_PEOPLE_REFERENCE.md

## Purpose

`distribution_through_people` contains durable mechanics for gaining distribution through customers, employees, creators, partners, communities, affiliates, advocates, referrals, collaborations, and other people who carry attention or trust into a new audience.

## Canonical boundaries

- Generic proof, trust, positioning, hooks, CTA, offer, funnel, qualification, sales, and content rules remain in their existing packs.
- This pack owns the mechanics created specifically by a third party carrying the message, introduction, endorsement, or audience access.
- `offer_framing` owns commercial terms of the core offer; this pack only owns partner/referral incentive design insofar as it changes distribution behavior.
- `funnel_mechanics` owns what happens after the introduced person enters the business funnel.
- `channel_ops` will own platform-specific affiliate tooling, referral software, attribution implementation, creator-platform rules, API behavior, current disclosure requirements, and channel-specific operational tactics.
- Community-building principles are included only where they directly create advocacy or people-powered distribution; generic community operations are out of scope.

## Durable model

**valuable experience / aligned audience → credible advocate or partner → low-friction share or introduction → transferred trust → trackable entry → downstream value → reciprocal reinforcement → deeper or broader distribution**

## Explicit non-rules

- every customer should be paid to refer
- cash is always the best referral incentive
- more affiliates always means more revenue
- bigger partner audience is always better
- every partnership must be reciprocal one-for-one
- referrals are free and therefore need no economics
- word of mouth cannot be measured
- every collaborator needs the same script
- community automatically creates advocacy
- incentives can compensate for a weak product or poor customer experience
- referral links alone create a referral program
- one successful super-affiliate is sufficient diversification
