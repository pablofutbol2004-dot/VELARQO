# CAROUSEL_REFERENCE.md

## Purpose

`carousel` contains mechanics created specifically by a user-controlled swipe sequence: static-cover entry, slide jobs and ordering, small-size visual hierarchy, recurring visual systems, sequence rhythm, standalone slide shareability, payoff/action placement, and carousel-level diagnosis.

## Canonical boundaries

- Generic hook construction remains in `hooks`; this pack owns the fact that a carousel must earn entry from a static first panel.
- Generic visual hierarchy, typography, contrast, composition, and design principles remain in `visual_communication`; this pack owns how those principles behave across small swipe panels and a recurring sequence.
- Evidence selection remains in `proof`; this pack only covers placement/integration created by the slide surface.
- CTA psychology remains in `cta_conversion`; this pack only covers where an action naturally sits after a completed swipe sequence.
- Idea/reference mining remains in `ideation_angles`; this pack may use references, but does not re-own the remix method.
- Platform-specific carousel limits, algorithms, audio behavior, dimensions, grid cropping, and distribution mechanics belong in future `channel_ops`.

## Durable model

**static entry → confirmed promise → ordered slide jobs → progressive payoff → relevant continuation → chain-level learning**

## Supplemental user source

Pass 11 also used the user-provided transcript **How I Create Visual Hooks** (`Ddn_xrtCxEE`) and the attached carousel visual-system transcript as high-signal supplemental evidence. Tool/vendor instructions, performance claims, and platform-specific prescriptions were not promoted as durable craft.

## Explicit non-rules

- every carousel must use exactly five beats
- every cover must be abstract or metaphorical
- every cover must contain one subject
- exactly three colors or exactly two fonts always win
- every slide must be independently shareable
- the product must always appear on slide three
- the CTA must always be the final slide
- carousels always convert better than reels/video
- one fixed slide count or page-number system
