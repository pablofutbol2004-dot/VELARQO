# WRITTEN_POST_REFERENCE.md

## Purpose

`written_post` contains mechanics created by text-first feed reading: visible-preview entry, paragraph and line-break navigation, text-only pacing, post-length progression, thread/multi-post chunking, optional attachments, dialogue continuation, and written-post-specific diagnosis.

## Canonical boundaries

- Idea discovery and point-of-view mining remain in `ideation_angles`.
- Generic hook construction remains in `hooks`; this pack owns the preview/expansion constraint of a written feed.
- Generic copy clarity, voice, storytelling, proof, value communication, and CTA principles remain in their general craft packs.
- Funnel routing and business outcomes remain in `funnel_mechanics`.
- Platform algorithms, character limits, hashtag/link behavior, exact preview truncation, recommended posting times, and current LinkedIn/X mechanics belong in future `channel_ops`.

## Durable model

**visible preview → one central thought → readable paragraph progression → completed point → dialogue or relevant next action → outcome-level learning**

## Explicit non-rules

- short posts always beat long posts
- long posts always build more authority
- every sentence should be its own paragraph
- every post needs an image
- every post needs a CTA
- controversy is required for reach
- one universal hook template
- threads should fill every available character
- impressions or likes alone define success
