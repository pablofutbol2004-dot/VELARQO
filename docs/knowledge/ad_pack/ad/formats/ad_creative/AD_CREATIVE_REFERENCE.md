# AD_CREATIVE_REFERENCE.md

## Purpose

`ad_creative` contains mechanics created specifically by paid creative: interruption-context entry, concept/angle/variation separation, paid-format choice, native presentation, compressed persuasion, creative testing, fatigue/portfolio management, and ad-specific diagnostic metrics.

## Canonical boundaries

- Generic hooks remain in `hooks`; this pack owns the paid-placement interruption and creative-opening constraints.
- Generic copywriting, visual communication, proof, offer framing, CTA, storytelling, and value communication remain in their existing packs.
- Generic causal testing and measurement principles remain in `content_measurement`; this pack owns ad-specific test units (concept vs variation) and local creative diagnostics such as hook/hold/CTR.
- Funnel stages, attribution, qualification, sales conversion, and destination-page mechanics remain in their business/format packs.
- Campaign structure, bidding, optimization events, targeting controls, attribution windows, platform algorithms, ad-policy implementation, and exact benchmark thresholds belong in future `channel_ops` and should be re-checked when used.

## Durable model

**market/persona → concept/angle → format/execution → paid interruption → compressed persuasion → congruent handoff → paid test → iteration/portfolio learning**

## Explicit non-rules

- video always beats static
- static always beats video
- UGC always beats polished creative
- ads should always look organic
- one universal hook-rate / hold-rate / CTR benchmark
- more creative volume is always better
- long-running competitor ads are guaranteed winners
- copy competitors verbatim
- every ad needs a testimonial
- every ad should contain the full sales argument
- one winner should receive all spend forever
- one fixed creative quota per week or month
