# LONG_FORM_VIDEO_REFERENCE.md

## Purpose

`long_form_video` contains mechanics created by sustained sequential video: choosing when depth justifies the commitment, macro-section architecture, repeated recommitment across a long runtime, chapter/selective-navigation decisions, recording mode, uneven edit/visual density, continuation into an adjacent long-form library, and long-runtime measurement.

## Canonical boundaries

- Topic/angle selection stays in `ideation_angles`.
- Titles/thumbnails and pre-click packaging stay in `packaging`.
- General opening/hook mechanics stay in `hooks`.
- General attention, curiosity, pacing, storytelling, spoken writing, visual communication, editing, proof, CTA, and measurement principles stay in their general craft packs.
- Offer design, funnel routing, qualification, and sales-conversation mechanics stay in business-mechanics packs.
- YouTube recommendation-system claims, end-screen implementation, cards, upload settings, SEO, current analytics definitions, and platform-specific benchmarks stay in future high-volatility `channel_ops` and must be re-checked at use time.

## Durable model

**format fit → macro skeleton → section-level value/recommitment → appropriate navigation → planned-but-natural recording → subtractive/selective edit → satisfied current promise → relevant continuation → timestamp-level learning**

## Explicit non-rules

The corpus contains many fixed prescriptions that were not promoted into durable rules, including:

- one universal long-form runtime
- one universal intro length
- cuts or pattern interrupts every fixed number of seconds
- chapters always improve or always hurt performance
- every long-form video needs one giant withheld payoff at the end
- every video must be fully scripted word-for-word
- every long-form video should be heavily edited
- percentage viewed alone determines video quality
- watch time alone explains recommendation performance
- every ending should push a platform end screen
- every channel should optimize for binge sessions regardless of viewer job

## Date-sensitive material excluded

YouTube recommendation mechanics, end-screen/card behavior, chapters/search effects, platform analytics definitions, CTR/AVD benchmarks, upload features, and current channel-growth tactics must be verified when used.
