# Installer outbound reply routing

## Positive / curious
Goal: answer directly, then move to 15–20 minute diagnostic call.

**“How does it work?”**
> You send a sample/export of old unclosed quotes, I check what’s actually usable, we agree the message and qualification rules, and we follow up the eligible contacts in your name. Interested homeowners get booked back into your diary. For the pilot I’m charging per qualified booking, not a retainer.

**“How much?”**
> For the founder pilot it’s £0 setup and £75 per qualified booked survey. A genuine no-show is credited/replaced. Before I’d ask you to run anything, I’d want to see roughly how many usable old quotes you have and how you store them.

**“Send info.”**
Send a one-screen summary, not a deck. End with one concrete question: `roughly how many old unclosed quotes do you have?`

## Skeptical
**“We already follow up.”**
> Makes sense. The useful question is whether every quote actually gets followed until there’s a clear yes/no/not-now outcome. If your team already does that consistently, this probably isn't useful. How many touches normally happen after a quote goes out?

**“Tried agencies.”**
> I’m not asking you to buy more leads or pay a retainer for activity. The pilot is on enquiries you already own and the billing unit is a qualified survey booking. If the old list isn't usable or nothing books, that's useful to learn quickly too.

**“GDPR?”**
> That matters before messaging. I don’t treat every old list as sendable. We check how the contact was collected, the original opt-out/marketing position and suppressions before a campaign. If the list doesn't pass that gate, we don't send it.

## Negative / opt-out
- acknowledge once if needed;
- suppress immediately;
- do not “handle the objection” after an explicit unsubscribe;
- store suppression at contact/domain/entity level as appropriate.

## Ambiguous
If the reply is legally sensitive, angry, asks for data-source details, threatens complaint, or questions deletion/access rights: route to human. Do not let an AI improvise.
