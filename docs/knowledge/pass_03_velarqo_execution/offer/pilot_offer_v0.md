# Pilot offer v0

## One sentence
**We follow up the window and door quotes you already paid to generate, identify the homeowners who are still interested, and put qualified surveys back in your diary.**

## Founder pilot
- **£0 setup**
- **£75 per qualified booked survey** — TEST PRICE for first 1–2 proof clients
- if no valid survey books, client pays £0
- genuine no-show/cancellation before survey = credit/replacement, not billable twice
- no long-term contract
- bounded first campaign
- client signs off messaging
- transparent contact/result ledger

## Qualified booked survey — v0 definition
Billable only when all are true:
1. contact originated from the client's eligible first-party database;
2. homeowner confirms current interest in a service the installer actually offers;
3. property is within agreed service area;
4. contact has not already bought / been re-opened by the client's team before campaign attribution;
5. a specific survey/consultation date/time (or client-defined equivalent) is agreed;
6. valid contact details exist;
7. booking is recorded in the agreed calendar/CRM/ledger.

### Not billable
- raw positive reply;
- “send me info” with no agreed next step;
- obvious wrong number/person;
- outside service area;
- unsupported product;
- duplicate already active in pipeline;
- explicit opt-out;
- appointment manufactured without affirmative homeowner agreement.

## No-show rule
For founder pilots:
- homeowner cancels before attendance → not separately billable if rebooked; one result remains one result;
- genuine no-show → automatic credit/replacement unless client caused it (wrong address, missed appointment, unavailable rep, etc.);
- disputed cases resolved from message + booking log, not memory.

## Client responsibilities
- truthful data/provenance information;
- approve message/offer;
- provide calendar/availability;
- respond to warm exceptions/escalations;
- update attended/quoted/won status;
- honour any promise/discount/offer in the campaign;
- respect opt-outs and Velarqo suppression list.

## What we do
- inspect/clean/segment eligible data;
- configure campaign;
- send approved messages;
- stop/suppress appropriately;
- triage routine replies;
- qualify and book within agreed scope;
- attribute every result;
- report outcomes.

## What we explicitly do not promise yet
- X% recovery rate;
- X appointments per 1,000 records;
- revenue guarantee;
- “AI will close customers”;
- campaignability of records whose original collection cannot be evidenced.

## Natural expansion only after proof
1. ongoing follow-up of every new quote;
2. missed-call / speed-to-lead;
3. no-show reminders;
4. review/referral workflows;
5. past-customer cross-sell where lawful/relevant.
