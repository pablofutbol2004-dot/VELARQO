# Commercial terms checklist — send to solicitor/gestor for final drafting

This is a deal-logic checklist, **not legal boilerplate**.

## Scope
- exact service
- channels used
- client approvals
- excluded activities

## Result definition
- “qualified booked survey” definition incorporated verbatim
- when result becomes billable
- no-show/cancellation/rebooking treatment
- duplicates/pre-existing opportunities
- invalid/out-of-area jobs
- attribution evidence
- attribution window

## Fees
- founder-pilot £/result
- VAT/tax treatment wording confirmed
- invoice cadence
- payment due date
- disputed-result process

## Client obligations
- lawful/provenance representations about data
- accurate suppression lists
- timely approvals
- calendar capacity
- honour advertised offers
- outcome updates
- access credentials/security responsibilities

## Data protection
- controller/processor roles
- processing subject matter/duration/purpose/data categories
- documented instructions
- confidentiality
- security
- approved sub-processors
- data-subject assistance
- breach process
- deletion/return at termination
- audit/information obligations
- international transfers if any

## Marketing compliance
- client confirms eligibility/lawful basis/PECR route for supplied contacts
- Velarqo may refuse/quarantine uncertain records
- opt-out/suppression handling
- client cannot instruct Velarqo to bypass suppression

## Liability / termination
- sensible liability cap to draft professionally
- excluded indirect losses where appropriate
- termination for convenience / breach
- immediate suspension for compliance/reputation risk
- data return/deletion
- outstanding valid result fees

## IP / systems
- client owns client data
- clarify ownership/licence of templates, workflows and software
- access revoked at termination

Have a qualified professional turn this logic into the actual agreement before treating it as finished legal text.
