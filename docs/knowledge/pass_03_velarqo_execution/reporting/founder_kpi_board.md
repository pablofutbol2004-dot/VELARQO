# Founder KPI board

## Acquisition — weekly
1. qualified prospects attempted
2. positive replies
3. qualified calls held
4. pilots/clients won
5. acquisition cash collected

Derived:
- positive reply rate
- call/positive-reply rate
- win/call rate
- CAC (cash + tools; track founder hours separately)

## Delivery — per client
1. eligible contacts
2. positive homeowner replies
3. qualified bookings
4. attended surveys
5. jobs won / revenue won when known

Derived:
- positive reply/contact
- booked/contact
- show rate
- sale/attended
- revenue/contact
- cost/booked
- manual minutes/booked

## Business health
- MRR / performance revenue
- gross margin after SMS/tools/contractors
- cash collected
- active clients
- client concentration
- churn/expansion once enough history exists

## Constraint rule
Every Friday identify the **single dominant bottleneck**:
- not enough prospects;
- weak positive reply;
- weak call conversion;
- data ineligible;
- campaign reply weak;
- booking weak;
- client no-shows/slow response;
- delivery too manual;
- retention/list exhaustion.

Work on that constraint before adding another system.
