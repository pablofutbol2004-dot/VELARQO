# 20-minute discovery call v0

Goal: diagnose whether a profitable, lawful pilot exists. Do not spend 20 minutes pitching.

## 0–2 min — frame
> I mainly want to understand how enquiries and quotes move through the business, what happens after a quote goes quiet, and what the old data actually looks like. If there isn't a sensible pilot, I'll tell you.

## 2–7 min — funnel numbers
Ask for approximate real numbers:
1. enquiries/month?
2. surveys/appointments/month?
3. quotes sent/month?
4. jobs won/month?
5. average order value?
6. roughly what does a normal job leave after materials/fitters/direct job cost? If they won't share margin, ask what a new sale is worth enough to spend to acquire.
7. main lead sources and approximate spend/cost?

Compute live:
- quote→sale rate;
- value of one additional sale;
- current cost per acquired sale where possible.

## 7–11 min — follow-up process
- What happens the day after a quote is sent?
- Then what?
- How many follow-ups normally happen?
- Who owns it?
- What causes follow-up to stop?
- What reasons do people give for not buying?
- Are there quiet months where this hurts more?

## 11–14 min — backlog/data
- How many unclosed quotes/enquiries roughly?
- How far back does it go?
- CRM, quoting software, spreadsheet, inbox, paper?
- Name/phone/email/quote date/value/status available?
- Can they export it?
- Ask for a redacted 20–50 row sample after call.

## 14–16 min — compliance/data provenance
- How do homeowners normally give details?
- website form / phone / showroom / purchased leads?
- Does the form/process include a marketing opt-out or preference?
- Where are opt-outs kept?
- Any bought lists mixed into the CRM?

Do not provide a legal verdict on the call. This is evidence collection.

## 16–18 min — operational fit
- Who will take/attend the recovered survey?
- How quickly can they act when someone re-engages?
- What areas/products should be excluded?
- How many additional surveys could they actually handle next week?

## 18–20 min — proposal if fit
> Based on that, I'd start small rather than blast the whole database. Give me a redacted sample/export and the original lead-capture wording. I’ll tell you what looks usable. If it passes the gate, we run a bounded pilot. £0 setup, £75 per qualified booked survey for the founder pilot, and genuine no-shows are credited. If nothing valid books, there’s nothing to pay.

Then agree the single next action and date.

## Disqualifiers
- cannot identify how data was collected;
- wants scraped/bought consumer list used as “reactivation”;
- tiny backlog with poor unit economics;
- no way to export/access data and no economically sensible workaround;
- cannot service more demand;
- refuses to honour opt-outs;
- wants fake discounts/urgency;
- will not update appointment outcomes;
- product/job economics too small for performance fee.
