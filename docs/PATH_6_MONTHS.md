## 3. Why Velarqo can win (from the competitor teardown)

- **Nobody sells this as their whole offer.** Lead Pronto, Leads 2 Trade and
  the ad agencies sell fresh leads. Software (Tradify £37, Powered Now £40,
  Sternfenster EasyAdmin+) sends templated reminders. ProfitWize does generic
  AI SMS with no installer language. The one agency using the old-quote angle
  (Glasgrowth) uses it as an opener for an ads retainer.
- **Nobody publishes a price, a guarantee or named proof.** You can, once
  there's one result.
- **Your line:** "Not an agency, no ads, no retainer. I chase the quotes
  you've already paid for, in your name, and put the ones still keen in your
  diary. You only pay for surveys that get booked." Signed with your full
  name and face.

   and English-speaking students first.
2. **Automation builds for small agencies** (3-6 weeks): package three
   fixed-price builds you already have (lead-list scraper, inbox/CRM
   workflow, monitoring scraper); 15-20 hand-written messages to small UK/ES
   agencies; paid pilot €300-500. Never from the Velarqo mailboxes.
3. Kill rule: no paid work by day 14 → tutoring and renders only.

**Legal:** monthly invoicing is "habitual" in Spain (STS 941/2025), so the
clean route is RETA with the tarifa plana (~€88/month). Book the gestor
now; register the week the first invoice (either track) is in sight. Avoid
cooperativas de facturación.
