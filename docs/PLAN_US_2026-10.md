## What we sell

**Estimate follow-up, done for you, for US home-improvement contractors.**

1. **The way in (pilot, 30 days):** we follow up the client's unsold estimates from the last 2-9 months, by email (and by text only where their forms collected consent). Anyone still interested is booked back onto their calendar. 20% of estimates are held out so the result can be proved.
2. **The monthly product:**
   - Every new estimate followed up until it's a yes or a no.
   - "Not now" leads checked in on every 1-3 months.
   - The backlog worked again each quarter.
   - A one-page report each month that compares results against the holdout.
3. **Added later, only once a client trusts us:** speed-to-lead on new enquiries, missed-call text-back, review requests.

**Price (to test on calls):**
- **First 2-3 clients:** a performance trial. Their first batch is free, and we're paid only if appointments come back.
- **After that, two options on every call:**
  - A: $500 for the pilot, then $750 a month.
  - B: pay per booked appointment ($100-200).
- **Fee rule:** never more than about 20% of the client's monthly profit.
- **Exclusivity:** one client per trade per city.

## Who

**Independent US firms** (no national chains) with 10+ staff or 100+ Google reviews, in East Coast and Central states first.

**Trade order:**
1. Kitchen & bath remodelers.
2. Window & door replacement.
3. Roofing: retail replacement only, no storm/insurance work, as a smaller test.

## How we get clients

- **Cold email** from the warmed inboxes. Email 1 plus one follow-up.
- **The free hook:** the 3 follow-up emails we'd send their quiet estimates, written in their company's name.
- **The call:** a 15-30 minute video call booked through a link, with slots 15:00-21:00 Spain time.
- **Later:** partners (supplier reps, CRM marketplaces) from week 2 or 3, and letters to dream firms after the first sale.
- **Credibility:** a LinkedIn profile and a simple US-facing page, both before the first send.

## Legal guardrails

- **Email (CAN-SPAM):**
  - The real sender name and business.
  - A valid postal address in every email.
  - A working opt-out, honoured permanently.
  - No deceptive subject lines.
- **Spanish law (LSSI):** the risk is accepted (see the legal memory).
- **Texts:** only to contacts whose form included consent. Some states have stricter text laws.
- **Calls by the client's staff:** Do Not Call list rules apply to estimates older than about 3 months. Check before any calling.
- **Client data:** a short data agreement for each pilot. Delete data 30 days after a pilot that doesn't continue.

## The test and its gates

- **Send:** about 1,000 firms per trade from early November. Remodelers and windows first, roofing smaller.
- **20 Nov:**
  - **Pass:** bounces under 3%, at least 5 interested replies, and at least 2 owners confirming dozens of quiet estimates.
  - **If a trade has 1-4 positives:** run it to 3,000 sends before judging it.
  - **If a trade has 0 positives from 1,000 firms:** change the hook, not the trade.
- **By 31 Jan:** at least 1 pilot run against a holdout.
  - **If it brings back under 1 job:** lead with new-estimate follow-up instead of the backlog.
  - **If fewer than 30% of pilots go monthly:** rethink the model.

## Maths we're testing (guesses until measured)

**Getting clients:** clients per 1,000 firms = replies × % positive × % booked × % showed × % closed.
- Base case: about 1.2 clients.
- Kill line: under 0.3.

**Results for a client:** jobs recovered = unsold estimates × % with an email × % who book again × % who close.
- Base case: about 2 jobs per pilot.
- One job ($10-20k) covers the fee many times over.
